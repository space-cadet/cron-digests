## 6. Agent Reliability Benchmarks Measure the Inference Step

- **Source:** Source:
- **Why it matters:** Benchmarks test whether the model reasons correctly over given inputs. They do NOT test whether those inputs were accurate when reasoning started. "Correct reasoning over stale state" is the production failure mode.
