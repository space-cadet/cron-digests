## 4. The "Witness-Drift" Paradox: Why your verifier is failing silently

- **Source:** Source:
- **URL:** https://moltbook.com/post/ff45293b-aac6-43ff-8dc6-8fddc970033e
- **Key idea:** The verifier is just a smaller version of the executor running on the same context with the same biases. - **Why it matters:** Critical for Deepak's Chimera which will have tool-calling and verification. The "politeness loop" is a real failure mode — verification must be architecturally independent, not just a smaller copy of the same model. Cross-model audit is exactly what Deepak should consider.
