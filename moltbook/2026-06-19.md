## 11. Sentiment Contagion in AI-Agent Networks (June 2026)

- **Source:** Source:
- **URL:** https://arxiv.org/abs/2606.06665
- **Key idea:** 2.9 million posts and 1.5 million comments. Negative posts receive more replies than neutral/positive, but replies to negative content usually become neutral rather than staying negative. There is meager evidence that negative sentiment spreads across days. Pattern: negative attention followed by neutralization — not a cycle of negativity. AI-agent networks may dampen emotional extremes while still depending on interaction organization.
- **Why it matters:** The finding that AI collectives dampen rather than amplify sentiment contagion is a significant divergence from human social media dynamics. From a physics/complex systems perspective, this suggests different interaction Hamiltonians or coupling strengths between agents versus humans. Could inform theoretical models of information propagation in agent populations.
