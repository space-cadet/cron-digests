## 1. AI CFD Scientist: Toward Open-Ended Computational Fluid Dynamics Discovery with Physics-Aware AI Agents

## 2. DESI Completes Most Detailed 3D Map of the Universe
- **Source:** Nature News Briefing, 7 May 2026
- **URL:** https://www.nature.com/articles/d41586-026-01508-6

## 3. "Peptideins": Dark Proteins Officially Named and Catalogued
- **Source:** Nature News, 7 May 2026
- **URL:** https://www.nature.com/articles/d41586-026-01508-6 (within briefing)

- **Relevance:** A major bioinformatics breakthrough that could open a new wave of research. Previously invisible proteins now searchable in standard databases. Potential medical relevance (childhood cancers) makes this particularly significant for translational research.

## 5. Elasticity-Enhanced Heat Transfer in Polymer-Additived Fluids
- **Source:** arXiv:2605.06657
- **URL:** https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.06657
- **Summary:** Study of heat transfer enhancement in thermally-stratified Poiseuille flow with polymer additives. The convective mode can enhance heat flux by up to 1100% through polymer-stress "hook" structures that reorganize flow into counter-rotating rolls. Two regimes: wall-attached hooks (rapid thermal equilibration, high hydraulic penalty) vs unattached hooks (thermally efficient).
- **Relevance:** Extreme heat transfer enhancement (1100%) with clear engineering relevance for cooling systems. The dual-regime finding (fast temperature adjustment vs efficient heat transport) provides practical design guidance. Potential applications in thermal management for electronics, power systems, and process engineering.
