## 1. An Algebraic Resolution of the Firewall Paradox
- **Authors:** Naman Kumar
- **arXiv ID:** 2605.14794
- **URL:** https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.14794
- **Categories:** hep-th, gr-qc
- **Summary:** The paper argues that the AMPS firewall paradox fails in diffeomorphism-invariant quantum gravity because gravitational dressing and asymptotic constraints prevent the tensor-product factorization of physical observables. Using modular theory and half-sided modular inclusions, the author shows that the interior Hawking partner algebra cannot be an independent commuting subalgebra, but must be contained within the radiation algebra. This undermines the entanglement-monogamy step of the paradox, suggesting that unitary evaporation and smooth horizons are compatible without invoking islands or replica wormholes.
- **Relevance:** A direct and conceptually sharp attack on the black-hole information paradox. It reframes the firewall debate in operator-algebraic terms and offers a pathway to reconcile unitarity with semiclassical physics in asymptotically flat quantum gravity.
- **Tags:** Black Hole Thermodynamics, Quantum Gravity, Information Paradox

## 2. Parametrization of the primordial power spectrum in loop quantum cosmology
- **Authors:** Almudena Guillén, Kai Langer, Guillermo A. Mena Marugán, Niklas Rodenbücher, Antonio Vicente-Becerril
- **arXiv ID:** 2605.14657
- **URL:** https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.14657
- **Categories:** gr-qc, astro-ph.CO, hep-th
- **Summary:** The authors derive a new parametrization of the primordial power spectrum for pre-inflationary bounce scenarios in loop quantum cosmology (hybrid and dressed metric approaches). The parametrization depends on the number of e-folds during the bounce and a characteristic suppression scale determined by the energy density at the bounce (which itself depends on the Immirzi parameter and the LQG area gap). Using the NO-AHD vacuum proposal, they find that for ~140 total e-folds both approaches agree well with Planck data at high multipoles and may improve the fit at low multipoles relative to ΛCDM.
- **Relevance:** A concrete, observationally testable bridge between loop quantum gravity and cosmology. The parametrization could help constrain LQG parameters using CMB data, making it highly relevant for quantum cosmology phenomenology.
- **Tags:** Loop Quantum Gravity, Quantum Cosmology, CMB Phenomenology

## 3. q-Askey Deformations of Double-Scaled SYK
- **Authors:** Sergio E. Aguilar-Gutierrez, Trivko Kukolj, Josef Seitz
- **arXiv ID:** 2605.13956
- **URL:** https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.13956
- **Categories:** hep-th, gr-qc, quant-ph
- **Summary:** The authors construct families of deformations of the double-scaled SYK model whose bulk interpretation involves transfer matrices encoding q-Askey orthogonal polynomials. In the semiclassical finite-temperature limit, Krylov complexity corresponds to the length of an Einstein-Rosen bridge connecting an End-Of-The-World brane to an AdS boundary. The models exhibit discrete energy levels signaling a new geometric transition in sine dilaton gravity, and the operator algebras can be type II_1 or type I_∞ factors with entanglement entropy manifesting via the Ryu-Takayanagi formula.
- **Relevance:** SYK remains a central toy model for quantum gravity and holography. This work connects quantum information metrics (Krylov complexity) to geometric transitions and baby universes, making it a strong candidate for anyone tracking holographic quantum gravity.
- **Tags:** Holography, String Theory, Quantum Information

## 4. C-Phase-Aware Compilation for Efficient Fault-Tolerant Quantum Execution
- **Authors:** Dhanvi Bharadwaj, Siddharth Dangwal, Yuewen Hou, Gokul Subramanian Ravi
- **arXiv ID:** 2605.14042
- **URL:** https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.14042
- **Categories:** quant-ph
- **Summary:** The paper presents a microarchitecture-aware compilation approach for fault-tolerant quantum computing that integrates algorithmic structure directly with lattice surgery execution. By exploiting the commutativity of C-Phase operations, the method transforms sequential gate sequences into concurrent multi-target interactions, removing artificial dependencies. An event-driven scheduling strategy models spatial layout and routing constraints, achieving up to 59.7× reduction in execution time compared to standard baselines.
- **Relevance:** Fault-tolerant compilation is a critical bottleneck on the path to practical quantum advantage. This work directly targets resource overheads in lattice-surgery-based FTQC and demonstrates substantial latency improvements, making it highly relevant for quantum computing engineering.
- **Tags:** Quantum Computing, Fault Tolerance, Quantum Compilation

## 5. Thermodynamics and optical aspects of ModMax black holes in higher order curvature gravity with quintessence dark energy
- **Authors:** Ahmad Al-Badawi, Usman Zafar, Abdul Jawad, Kazuharu Bamba
- **arXiv ID:** 2605.13908
- **URL:** https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.13908
- **Categories:** gr-qc, hep-th
- **Summary:** The authors derive an exact black hole solution in higher-order curvature gravity coupled to ModMax electrodynamics and quintessence dark energy. They analyze thermodynamic and geothermodynamic properties, showing that divergences in thermodynamic curvature coincide with vanishing heat capacity. They also study optical properties via null geodesics, finding exact photon-sphere radius expressions and showing that quintessence significantly enhances the shadow size—more so than electric charge—suggesting observable signatures of dark energy and curvature corrections in black hole shadows.
- **Relevance:** Connects black hole thermodynamics, modified gravity, and dark energy with observable astrophysical signatures (shadow geometry). A strong fit for readers interested in black hole phenomenology beyond general relativity.
- **Tags:** Black Hole Thermodynamics, Modified Gravity, Dark Energy

## 6. Fermi Surface Geometry from Charge Fluctuations in Three-Dimensional Metals
- **Authors:** Pok Man Tam, Yarden Sheffer, Xiao-Chuan Wu, F. D. M. Haldane, Shinsei Ryu
- **arXiv ID:** 2605.13951
- **URL:** https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.13951
- **Categories:** cond-mat.mes-hall, cond-mat.quant-gas, cond-mat.str-el, hep-th, quant-ph
- **Summary:** For 3D non-interacting multi-band metals, the authors show that the subleading logarithmic term of bipartite charge fluctuations encodes information about the shape and quantum geometry of Fermi surfaces. This term is related to a momentum-space structure-factor coefficient expressible as a Fermi surface integral of the curvature tensor and the quantum metric tensor. For quadric partition surfaces, the logarithmic coefficient satisfies a topological bound depending on the Euler characteristic and Chern number, revealing a nontrivial interplay between topology and quantum geometry.
- **Relevance:** A beautiful theoretical result linking quantum geometry, topology, and measurable charge fluctuations in metals. It sits at the intersection of condensed matter and high-energy mathematical methods and offers new diagnostic tools for correlated electron systems.
- **Tags:** Condensed Matter, Quantum Geometry, Topology

## 7. A Tale of Two Hartle-Hawking Wave Functions: Fully Gravitational vs Partially Frozen
- **Authors:** Galit Anikeeva, Raphaël Dulac, Zixia Wei, Mengyang Zhang
- **arXiv ID:** 2605.13970
- **URL:** https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.13970
- **Categories:** hep-th, gr-qc
- **Summary:** The authors revisit the Hartle-Hawking wave function in AdS, contrasting a fully gravitational construction (boundary configuration integrated over) with a partially frozen one (boundary fixed, as in AdS/CFT). They evaluate one-loop corrections to the hyperbolic-ball partition function in D-dimensional AdS Einstein gravity, finding that the fully gravitational version develops a nontrivial one-loop phase of (∓i)^{D+1}, analogous to dS gravity, while the partially frozen version remains real and positive. They also investigate a partially frozen dS sphere partition function where the equator metric is fixed.
- **Relevance:** Provides a careful comparison between two constructions of the Hartle-Hawking wave function with implications for the phase problem in quantum cosmology and the interpretation of gravitational path integrals in AdS and dS.
- **Tags:** Quantum Cosmology, Quantum Gravity, AdS/CFT

## 8. Wormholes and Averaging over N
- **Authors:** Jonah Kudler-Flam, Edward Witten
- **arXiv ID:** 2605.15180
- **URL:** https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.15180
- **Categories:** hep-th
- **Summary:** The authors address how wormholes in the gravitational path integral can compute ensemble averages when typical AdS/CFT constructions have only a single integer N to average over. They introduce "Mellin averaging" to define an asymptotic average of a function of N, arguing it may suffice to reproduce wormhole randomness if the dual theory admits analytic continuation in N and observables fluctuate on superpolynomially small scales. They test this on the spectral form factor and discuss toy models of analytic continuation.
- **Relevance:** A high-profile paper touching on the foundational role of wormholes in holography and ensemble averages. The Mellin-averaging proposal is a novel conceptual tool with broad implications for understanding gravitational path integrals.
- **Tags:** Quantum Gravity, Holography, Wormholes

## 9. All-Electric Quantum State Transfer via Spin-Orbit Phase Matching
- **Authors:** Madhumita Sarkar, Roopayan Ghosh, Charles G. Smith, Maksym Myronov, Sougato Bose
- **arXiv ID:** 2605.13976
- **URL:** https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.13976
- **Categories:** quant-ph, cond-mat.str-el
- **Summary:** Semiconductor hole-spin qubits are promising for quantum computation due to weak hyperfine interaction and strong spin-orbit coupling enabling electric control. However, long-distance quantum state transfer is hindered by spin-orbit-induced anisotropic exchange. The authors show that this can be overcome by an all-electric control protocol: tuning electric field strength identifies discrete spin-orbit phase-matching conditions that restore near-perfect state transfer, while controlling field direction aligns the spin-orbit axis and suppresses non-conserving processes.
- **Relevance:** Addresses a key scalability challenge in semiconductor spin-qubit architectures. All-electric control without fine tuning is a significant step toward practical quantum information transport in solid-state platforms.
- **Tags:** Quantum Computing, Spin Qubits, Quantum Information

## 11. How Much Can Gravitons Be Squeezed?
- **Authors:** Panagiotis Dorlis, Nick E. Mavromatos, Sarben Sarkar, Sotirios-Neilos Vlachos
- **arXiv ID:** 2605.14797
- **URL:** https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.14797
- **Categories:** gr-qc, astro-ph.CO, hep-ph
- **Summary:** The authors propose an astrophysical mechanism to overcome Planck-scale suppression of quantum gravity effects: superradiant axion-like-particle clouds around rotating black holes can generate multimode squeezed states containing 10^6–10^7 correlated gravitons. These states exhibit distinctive polarization correlations and quantum-noise signatures potentially detectable in future gravitational-wave interferometers. Detection would constitute direct evidence for the quantum nature of gravitational radiation.
- **Relevance:** Proposes a concrete, observationally accessible route to detecting quantum properties of gravity. The connection between axion clouds and squeezing of gravitons makes this a unique entry point for quantum gravity phenomenology.

## 12. The Smarr Formula is Gauss's Law: A Kerr-Schild Single-Copy Perspective
- **Authors:** Gokhan Alkac
- **arXiv ID:** 2605.14707
- **URL:** https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.14707
- **Categories:** hep-th, gr-qc
- **Summary:** In the Kerr-Schild double copy, static spherically symmetric black hole solutions of general relativity map to purely electric solutions of Maxwell's theory. The author demonstrates that for these configurations, the thermodynamic Smarr formula is structurally identical to the single-copy Gauss's law. In asymptotically AdS spacetimes, the thermodynamic pressure-volume term naturally emerges from a gauge-theoretic background subtraction, establishing a novel connection between the classical double copy and black hole thermodynamics.
- **Relevance:** A concise and elegant result connecting the Kerr-Schild double-copy program to black-hole thermodynamics. It reinforces the surprising depth of the gravity/gauge-theory correspondence at the classical level.
- **Tags:** Black Hole Thermodynamics, Double Copy, Classical Gravity
