## 1. Physical inner product in LQG cosmology via covariant coarse graining
- **Authors:** M. Campiglia, R. Gambini, J. Pullin
- **arXiv ID:** 2605.12392
- **URL:** https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.12392
- **Categories:** gr-qc
- **Summary:** The construction of a physical inner product is a long-standing open problem in loop quantum gravity. This paper adapts recently developed covariant coarse-graining techniques—originally formulated in the spin-foam context—to the simpler setting of isotropic loop quantum cosmology. The authors identify a well-defined inner product on the reduced physical Hilbert space and verify its consistency. The approach opens a concrete path toward defining the physical inner product in full LQG by leveraging coarse-graining as a regulator.
- **Relevance:** Directly addresses the construction of the physical inner product in LQG, a foundational challenge. The isotropic cosmology simplification makes it a tractable proof of principle. Highly relevant to loop quantum gravity and quantum cosmology researchers.
- **Tags:** Quantum Gravity, Cosmology & Early Universe, Foundations & Interpretations

## 2. Neural Network Field Theory: Schwinger–Dyson equations, Ward identities, and anomalies
- **Authors:** P. O'Brien, E. Teh, D. Erhan, R. Easther
- **arXiv ID:** 2605.12488
- **URL:** https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.12488
- **Categories:** hep-th, cs.LG
- **Summary:** Neural network field theory (NN-FT) formulates field theory in terms of a network architecture and a parameter-space density. The authors derive Schwinger–Dyson equations and Ward identities in this framework, using them to study anomalies. A key result is a conserved parameter-space current that characterizes symmetries and their breaking—relevant even in non-local NN-FTs, recovering local currents for local Lagrangians. The formalism connects statistical mechanics of neural networks to quantum field theory symmetries.
- **Relevance:** Bridges high-energy theory and machine learning in a mathematically rigorous way. The anomaly analysis via parameter-space currents is novel. Relevant to researchers at the intersection of string theory/QFT and ML.
- **Tags:** Field Theory & Mathematical Physics, High-Energy Physics & Phenomenology, Quantum Information & Computing

## 3. Silicon color centers as telecom quantum memories: all-optical spin control
- **Authors:** F. Cache, K. V.R., T. Herzig, A. Yu. Kuznetsov, S. Pezzagna, M. Abbarchi, I. Robert-Philip, J.-M. Gérard, G. Cassabois, V. Jacques, A. Dréau
- **arXiv ID:** 2605.12473
- **URL:** https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.12473
- **Categories:** quant-ph, cond-mat.mtrl-sci
- **Summary:** The silicon G center—a telecom O-band single-photon emitter with an optically addressable metastable electron-spin triplet—is investigated as a quantum memory platform. The authors demonstrate all-optical initialization, readout, and coherent control of G-center spins using resonant and Raman protocols. Coherence times up to 1.4 ms at 2 K are achieved. The G center is argued to be competitive with T centers and group-IV donors for scalable silicon quantum photonics.
- **Relevance:** Silicon color centers are a leading candidate for quantum networking and distributed quantum computing. All-optical control at telecom wavelengths is a critical practical advance. Highly relevant to quantum computing hardware and quantum information.
- **Tags:** Quantum Information & Computing, Many-Body Physics & Condensed Matter, Technology & Engineering

## 4. Query-optimal quantum simulation of non-Hermitian Hamiltonians via bivariate QSP
- **Authors:** A. Courtney, A. Lund, E. Thamé, D. Jennings
- **arXiv ID:** 2605.12450
- **URL:** https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.12450
- **Categories:** quant-ph, math-ph
- **Summary:** The authors achieve query-optimal quantum simulations of non-Hermitian Hamiltonians of the form H_eff = H_R + iH_I using a bivariate extension of quantum signal processing (QSP) with non-commuting signal operators. The algorithm encodes the interaction-picture Dyson series as a polynomial on the bitorus, implemented through multivariable QSP (M-QSP). A constant-ratio condition guarantees scalar angle-finding for M-QSP circuits, enabling efficient compilation.
- **Relevance:** Non-Hermitian Hamiltonians appear in open quantum systems, dissipative engineering, and effective field theories. Query-optimal simulation is a significant algorithmic advance. Relevant to quantum algorithm designers and quantum information theorists.
- **Tags:** Quantum Information & Computing, Foundations & Interpretations, Field Theory & Mathematical Physics

## 5. Quasinormal spectra of fields in asymptotically de Sitter black holes in generalized Proca theory
- **Authors:** M. Skvortsova
- **arXiv ID:** 2605.12113
- **URL:** https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.12113
- **Categories:** gr-qc
- **Summary:** Massless scalar, electromagnetic, and Dirac perturbations of asymptotically de Sitter black holes in generalized Proca theory are studied. The Proca sector generates both a primary-hair parameter and an effective cosmological term, reshaping horizon structure. Quasinormal spectra are computed via Padé-improved WKB and time-domain integration. Key findings: the scalar sector (especially ℓ=0) is most sensitive to metric deformations; increasing the Proca-hair parameter weakens damping; and the de Sitter branch is resolved alongside the Schwarzschild-like branch.
- **Relevance:** Combines modified gravity (generalized Proca), black hole physics, and de Sitter cosmology. Quasinormal modes are observables for gravitational wave and black hole spectroscopy. Relevant to black hole thermodynamics and quantum gravity phenomenology.
- **Tags:** Black Hole Physics, Cosmology & Early Universe, Gravitational Waves & Astrophysics

## 6. Magnon polaritons in a van der Waals ferromagnet coupled to a superconducting resonator
- **Authors:** A. Bermejillo-Seco, L.J. van der Goot, M. Arfini, Y.M. Blanter, G.A. Steele, H.S.J. van der Zant
- **arXiv ID:** 2605.12298
- **URL:** https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.12298
- **Categories:** cond-mat.mes-hall
- **Summary:** Magnon-photon hybridization in the microwave regime is demonstrated in exfoliated flakes of the van der Waals ferromagnet Cr₂Ge₂Te₆ (thicknesses down to 30 nm), using a low-impedance superconducting resonator design. Reproducible avoided crossings across six devices evidence magnon polaritons. The coupling strength follows the expected square-root thickness dependence, and extrapolation indicates monolayer-limit hybridization is within reach.
- **Relevance:** Integrating 2D magnetic materials with superconducting circuits is a frontier in hybrid quantum systems. Van der Waals ferromagnets enable extreme miniaturization of magnon-based quantum devices. Relevant to condensed matter and quantum technology.
- **Tags:** Many-Body Physics & Condensed Matter, Quantum Information & Computing, Technology & Engineering

## 7. Topology-dependent criticality in triplet majority-rule dynamics with collective reversal
- **Authors:** R. Muslim
- **arXiv ID:** 2605.11692
- **URL:** https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.11692
- **Categories:** physics.soc-ph
- **Summary:** A triplet majority-rule opinion-dynamics model with collective reversal is studied on quenched networks (Barabási–Albert, Erdős–Rényi, random regular, Watts–Strogatz). The critical point shifts away from the well-mixed value for all networks, but critical exponents remain mean-field-like for BA/ER/RR. Watts–Strogatz networks exhibit much lower critical points and stronger exponent deviations, indicating that clustering and local correlations lead to topology-dependent effective critical behavior. Rewiring analysis shows the ordered phase stabilizes as randomness increases.
- **Relevance:** Opinion dynamics is a core social physics model. The finding that network topology can modify critical exponents—not just shift the critical point—is significant for understanding how social network structure affects collective phenomena. Relevant to sociophysics and complex networks.
- **Tags:** Phase Transitions & Critical Phenomena, Science Communication & Policy

## 8. Classifying double copies and multicopies in AdS
- **Authors:** V. Didenko, A. Dosmanbetov
- **arXiv ID:** 2605.12359
- **URL:** https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.12359
- **Categories:** hep-th
- **Summary:** The double-copy relations connecting gauge and gravity theories are extended to multicopies in anti-de Sitter space. The authors classify higher-spin gauge theories in AdS₄ that admit double-copy descriptions, identifying the subset that also admit multicopy generalizations. The analysis connects to Vasiliev's higher-spin gravity and the CHS (conformal higher-spin) theories, providing a systematic framework for constructing gravity-like theories from gauge-theory building blocks in curved backgrounds.
- **Relevance:** The double copy is one of the most surprising structures in quantum field theory and string theory, with direct connections to AdS/CFT and higher-spin gravity. Extending it to AdS and multicopies opens new avenues for constructing and understanding quantum gravity. Relevant to string theory and holography.
- **Tags:** High-Energy Physics & Phenomenology, Field Theory & Mathematical Physics, Quantum Gravity
