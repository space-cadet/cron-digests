## 1. Negative entropy in scrambling black holes
- **Authors:** Koji Azuma
- **arXiv ID:** 2605.25315
- **URL:** https://arxivite.org/abs/2605.25315
- **Categories:** hep-th; gr-qc; quant-ph
- **Summary:** Presents a microscopic statistical-mechanical foundation for interpreting the horizon area of a scrambling black hole as coherent information — equivalently negative conditional quantum entropy — within Hawking's pair-creation picture. Derives the entropy increase induced when an infalling object is absorbed and scrambled into the black hole's microscopic degrees of freedom, and shows that up to finite-resolution corrections, the black hole entropy can be identified with the negative conditional entropy of the created pairs.
- **Relevance:** Connects black hole thermodynamics directly with quantum information theory via the concept of negative entropy in scrambling dynamics. Relevant to the black hole information paradox and the quantum mechanical nature of horizon entropy.
- **Tags:** black-holes, entropy, quantum-information, scrambling, information-paradox

## 4. Non-monotonic evolution of multipartite entanglement under the Unruh effect
- **Authors:** Shu-Min Wu, Si-Han Shang, Si-Yu Liu, Rui-Yang Xu, Qianqian Liu, Xiao-Li Huang
- **arXiv ID:** 2605.24424
- **URL:** https://arxivite.org/abs/2605.24424
- **Categories:** gr-qc; quant-ph
- **Summary:** Investigates tetrapartite entanglement in a four-qubit Dicke state under relativistic motion using the Unruh-DeWitt detector model. Shows that entanglement exhibits a non-monotonic evolution — first decreasing and subsequently increasing toward a finite value as the acceleration grows — in contrast to the conventional monotonic degradation expected from the Unruh effect.
- **Relevance:** Reveals unexpected non-monotonic entanglement dynamics under relativistic acceleration, challenging naive expectations about the Unruh effect's impact on quantum correlations. Important for quantum information in curved spacetime and relativistic quantum communication.
- **Tags:** entanglement, unruh-effect, relativistic-quantum-information, multipartite, curved-spacetime

## 8. Hawking radiation from a semi-classical Schwarzschild black hole
- **Authors:** Moslem Shafiee, Ahmad Sheykhi
- **arXiv ID:** 2605.24487
- **URL:** https://arxivite.org/abs/2605.24487
- **Categories:** hep-th; gr-qc
- **Summary:** Investigates the evaporation of a Schwarzschild black hole incorporating quantum corrections from conformal anomaly and vacuum polarization. Shows that these corrections produce a maximum temperature after which Hawking radiation decreases and eventually ceases, leaving an extremal remnant with vanishing temperature and a logarithmically corrected entropy. Also examines the radiation via the tunneling method, providing a consistent picture of these semi-classical quantum effects.
- **Relevance:** Hawking radiation remains central to black hole thermodynamics and the information paradox. Semi-classical approaches offer a controlled setting for understanding quantum corrections to black hole evaporation.
- **Tags:** hawking-radiation, black-holes, semi-classical, quantum-field-theory

## 9. Multicritical points of gravitational solitons and a black hole in four dimensions
- **Authors:** Moaathe Belhaj Ahmed, Moisés Bravo-Gaete, Robert B. Mann, Constanza Quijada
- **arXiv ID:** 2605.24783
- **URL:** https://arxivite.org/abs/2605.24783
- **Categories:** hep-th; gr-qc
- **Summary:** Presents the first realization of multicritical points in four-dimensional general relativity within Plebański nonlinear electrodynamics using a polynomial structural function. Constructs new families of electrically charged asymptotically anti-de Sitter black holes and magnetically charged solitons, analyzing their thermodynamic properties in the grand-canonical ensemble. Shows that the soliton sector develops multiple swallowtail structures signaling first-order phase transitions, with the number of coexisting phases controlled by the degree of the polynomial.
- **Relevance:** Extends the thermodynamic analogy for black holes to multicritical phenomena, revealing rich phase structures beyond simple first- or second-order transitions. Relevant to holographic phase diagrams and the thermodynamic geometry of gravitational systems.
- **Tags:** black-holes, thermodynamics, multicritical, phase-transitions, solitons
