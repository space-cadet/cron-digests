- **arXiv ID:** 2605.08067
- **URL:** https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.08067
- **Relevance:** Investigates the mysterious 1/9 fractional magnetization plateau in the S=1/2 anisotropic kagome antiferromagnet Y-kapellasite using pulsed-field magnetization and high-field ³⁵Cl NMR. Identifies a hierarchy of field-induced fractional features including 1/3 and 1/9 plateaus. The 1/9 plateau is accompanied by an ordered local spin configuration and strong suppression of low-energy spin fluctuations, consistent with a gapped fractional state. This plateau shows surprising robustness to details of the underlying magnetism, differing from the only other known material exhibiting it. A significant experimental contribution to understanding quantum spin liquids and fractionalized excitations in geometrically frustrated magnets.

## 9. Two-Body Schrödinger–Newton Model: Entanglement Generation and Self-Localization
- **Authors:** Marcin Płodzień
- **arXiv ID:** 2605.06577 (cross-list from quant-ph)
- **URL:** https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.06577
- **Relevance:** Proposes a two-body Schrödinger-Newton model that separates local nonlinear self-localization from the nonseparable Newtonian pair potential. Analytically shows that the nonlinear self-field preserves the Schmidt spectrum while direct entanglement generation arises from the pair potential. Numerical simulations find that entanglement generation depends sensitively on initial spatial configuration and mass ratio. For dispersive Gaussian initial states, mass asymmetry produces Wigner negativity and rapid entanglement growth, whereas stationary SN profiles strongly suppress this. Provides insights into the interplay between gravitational self-interaction and quantum entanglement at the semiclassical gravity interface.
