## 1. Not all black holes decohere quantum superpositions
- **Authors:** Anna Biggs, Stefano Trezzi
- **arXiv ID:** 2605.23880
- **URL:** https://arxivite.org/abs/2605.23880
- **Categories:** hep-th, gr-qc, quant-ph
- **Summary:** Near-extremal charged black holes induce decoherence on quantum systems in their exterior, but this work shows that if the black hole is sufficiently close to extremality, quantum gravity effects make the decoherence rate vanish at late times. This phenomenon arises from a spin-induced energy gap in the quantum black hole spectrum. For energies above the gap, the decoherence rate is still suppressed relative to semiclassical expectations — quantum gravity effects always enhance the coherence of the superposition.
- **Relevance:** Directly connects black hole thermodynamics with quantum information, showing that quantum gravitational effects near extremality can protect rather than destroy quantum coherence. Relevant to black hole information paradox and quantum gravity phenomenology.
- **Tags:** black-holes, decoherence, near-extremal, quantum-gravity, information-paradox

## 2. Thermodynamics and transport in holographic QCD with Gauss-Bonnet corrections
- **Authors:** ChenWei Tong, Jie Zhou, YuanXu Wang, Rong-Gen Cai, Song He, Li Li
- **arXiv ID:** 2605.23834
- **URL:** https://arxivite.org/abs/2605.23834
- **Categories:** hep-th, gr-qc, hep-ph
- **Summary:** Investigates thermodynamics and transport in a holographic QCD model extending Einstein–Maxwell–Dilaton with Gauss–Bonnet corrections. Parameters are fixed using lattice QCD thermodynamics. For constant Gauss–Bonnet coupling, the model yields a temperature-dependent η/s but with monotonic behavior near the crossover. When the coupling depends on the dilaton, a non-monotonic η/s and peaked ζ/s emerge, with a critical end point in a phenomenologically relevant region of the phase diagram.
- **Relevance:** Holographic QCD provides a strong-coupling window into quark-gluon plasma physics. The transport coefficients and phase structure derived here are directly comparable to heavy-ion collision data and lattice QCD, bridging gravitational physics with nuclear phenomenology.
- **Tags:** holography, qcd, quark-gluon-plasma, transport-coefficients, phase-diagram

## 3. Dissipative non-Abelian fluids from Scherk-Schwarz dimensional reduction
- **Authors:** Emilio Torrente-Lujan
- **arXiv ID:** 2605.23842
- **URL:** https://arxivite.org/abs/2605.23842
- **Categories:** hep-th, gr-qc
- **Summary:** Constructs a d-dimensional dissipative colored fluid by Scherk–Schwarz reduction of a neutral viscous conformal fluid in D=d+n dimensions on an n-dimensional unimodular group manifold. Off-diagonal stress tensor components become non-Abelian color currents. The paper derives the full map for equation of state, sound speed, entropy current, and first-order transport coefficients, and shows how the second law descends from the parent theory, including temperature-dependent viscosity effects.
- **Relevance:** A rigorous holographic/hydrodynamic construction connecting higher-dimensional gravity with non-Abelian dissipative fluid dynamics. Provides a toy model for quark-gluon plasma with explicit transport coefficient formulas derived from dimensional reduction.
- **Tags:** holography, hydrodynamics, scherk-schwarz, non-abelian, quark-gluon-plasma

## 4. Entanglement entropy across the dynamical phase transition in the quantum O(N) model
- **Authors:** Frederick del Pozo, Tangi Morvan, Irénée Frérot, Nicolas Cherroret
- **arXiv ID:** 2605.23600
- **URL:** https://arxivite.org/abs/2605.23600
- **Categories:** quant-ph, cond-mat.other, cond-mat.quant-gas
- **Summary:** Demonstrates that the dynamical phase transition of the quantum O(N) model at large N leaves universal fingerprints in the infrared structure of the entanglement spectrum. While the leading entanglement entropy follows a volume law, subleading behavior distinguishes dynamical regimes. Quenches at and below the critical point generate gapless low-energy entanglement modes with logarithmic corrections governed by the dynamical exponent. The entanglement eigenmodes reveal characteristic signatures through their spatio-temporal structure and degeneracy properties.
- **Relevance:** Connects quantum critical dynamics with entanglement structure in a tractable large-N model. The scaling laws for entanglement across dynamical phase transitions are relevant to both quantum simulation and quantum gravity (via tensor network / holographic entanglement parallels).
- **Tags:** entanglement-entropy, dynamical-phase-transition, quantum-o-n, many-body, criticality

## 5. Coherent dynamics in chaotic spin chains via interference-protected subspaces
- **Authors:** Aron Kerschbaumer, Jean-Yves Desaules, Maksym Serbyn
- **arXiv ID:** 2605.23873
- **URL:** https://arxivite.org/abs/2605.23873
- **Categories:** quant-ph, cond-mat.quant-gas, cond-mat.stat-mech, cond-mat.str-el
- **Summary:** Introduces local spin-1/2 chains with structured subspaces hosting nonthermal phenomena — scars, chiral quasiparticles, and approximate topological edge modes — at high energy densities. These subspaces are protected by destructive interference. The authors develop a quantitative leakage theory predicting which states retain coherence, and connect asymptotic scars, quantum cages, and parent-Hamiltonian constructions, showing weak ergodicity breaking extends well beyond revivals of homogeneous states.
- **Relevance:** Quantum many-body scarring is a frontier in non-equilibrium dynamics. The interference-protection mechanism and its connection to topological edge modes offer a new lens on how coherence survives chaos, with implications for quantum simulation and thermalization physics.
- **Tags:** quantum-scars, many-body, weak-ergodicity-breaking, topological-edge-modes, spin-chains

## 6. Indefinite probabilities in quantum spacetime: A deepening of unpredictability
- **Authors:** Vittorio D'Esposito, Giuseppe Fabiano, Domenico Frattulillo
- **arXiv ID:** 2605.23862
- **URL:** https://arxivite.org/abs/2605.23862
- **Categories:** quant-ph, gr-qc
- **Summary:** Shows that employing the SU_q(2) quantum group to describe rotational symmetry in quantum spacetime leads to non-commuting probability operators for spin measurement outcomes, realizing a notion of "indefinite probabilities." This effect propagates to the rotation matrix between observers' reference frames, preventing them from jointly measuring their relative orientation — a fundamentally non-classical feature of quantum spacetime geometry.
- **Relevance:** A novel quantum-gravitational effect bridging quantum groups, quantum information, and spacetime non-commutativity. The framework connects to algebraic approaches in quantum gravity and suggests observable consequences for reference-frame physics in quantum spacetime.
- **Tags:** quantum-spacetime, quantum-groups, quantum-information, non-commutative-geometry

## 7. The negativity core of a 1+1D massless scalar quantum field
- **Authors:** Jason Pye, Atharva Hingane, Robert H. Jonsson
- **arXiv ID:** 2605.23824
- **URL:** https://arxivite.org/abs/2605.23824
- **Categories:** hep-th, quant-ph
- **Summary:** Provides a complete characterization of vacuum entanglement between two bounded spacelike-separated regions in a (1+1)-dimensional free massless real scalar field. Using Gaussian state methods, the authors analytically compute the logarithmic negativity and construct closed-form solutions for the localized modes carrying it, called "negativity cores." The results deepen understanding of entanglement in quantum field theory and suggest extensions to higher dimensions and fermionic fields.
- **Relevance:** Vacuum entanglement in quantum field theory is foundational for both quantum information and holography. The explicit analytical results in 1+1D provide a benchmark for entanglement measures in continuum quantum fields, with direct relevance to the ER=EPR conjecture and black hole entropy.
- **Tags:** entanglement, quantum-field-theory, negativity, vacuum-entanglement, holography

## 8. Interaction-Split Edge Spectral Flow and Neutral Triplet Boundary Modes in a C = 2 Hubbard Pump
- **Authors:** Yong-Feng Yang, Zhao-Rui Tian, Chen Cheng, Hong-Gang Luo
- **arXiv ID:** 2605.23769
- **URL:** https://arxivite.org/abs/2605.23769
- **Categories:** cond-mat.str-el
- **Summary:** A spatially modulated Hubbard interaction realizes a many-body Thouless pump with many-body Chern number C=+2. DMRG calculations identify a correlated insulator where the charge gap separates from the spin gap. Under open boundaries, the edge spectral flow splits into two distinct events, with a neutral, spinful triplet-like excitation localized between them. The phase diagram shows filling-dependent topology with transitions between C=+2, C=-2, and C=+1 topological Mott pumps.
- **Relevance:** Many-body topological pumps sit at the intersection of topology and strong correlations. The discovery of interaction-split spectral flow and neutral triplet boundary modes advances understanding of topological invariants in interacting fermionic systems, with connections to fractional quantum Hall physics.

## 9. Quantum Quenches that Resemble Operator Growth
- **Authors:** Xiangyu Cao
- **arXiv ID:** 2605.23874
- **URL:** https://arxivite.org/abs/2605.23874
- **Categories:** quant-ph, cond-mat.stat-mech
- **Summary:** Studies "growth quenches" in kinetic constrained models (East-West model) that destabilize false vacua, drawing formal analogies to operator growth in the Heisenberg picture. Using the Krylov approach, the author conjectures linear growth of Lanczos coefficients and derives bounds on a Lyapunov-like exponent. Fock localization is realized in SYK-inspired models, and caged states in 1D are interpreted as conserved charges with ballistic transport. Growth quenches are proposed as a platform for non-equilibrium coherent phenomena.
- **Relevance:** Operator growth and quantum chaos are central to modern many-body physics and black hole information. The formal analogy between quench dynamics and operator growth opens a new analytical window into scrambling and thermalization, connecting SYK physics with lattice models.

## 10. Probing Chaos and Criticality with Observational Entropy and Finite-Resolution Measurements
- **Authors:** J. Bharathi Kannan, Sreeram PG, M. S. Santhanam
- **arXiv ID:** 2605.23585
- **URL:** https://arxivite.org/abs/2605.23585
- **Categories:** quant-ph
- **Summary:** Shows that observational entropy (OE), defined from finite-resolution measurement outcomes, provides a unified framework for quantifying chaos and probing criticality. From the Aubry-Andre insulator-metal crossover to the destruction of KAM tori in the Kicked Rotor, derivatives of OE diagnose transitions accurately once resolution exceeds a finite threshold. In the chaotic limit, OE exhibits linear behavior within the Ehrenfest time regime, with slope defining an observable Lyapunov exponent that quantitatively reproduces the classical value.
- **Relevance:** Observational entropy bridges classical instability, quantum criticality, and realistic finite-resolution experiments. The framework is experimentally accessible and provides a compact information-theoretic diagnostic for chaos and phase transitions in quantum systems.
- **Tags:** observational-entropy, quantum-chaos, criticality, kicked-rotor, aubry-andre

## 12. Quantum resource redistribution drives spectral splits in dense neutrino gases
- **Authors:** Michael Hite, Pooja Siwach
- **arXiv ID:** 2605.23584
- **URL:** https://arxivite.org/abs/2605.23584
- **Categories:** quant-ph, hep-ph
- **Summary:** Characterizes the quantum resource landscape of collective neutrino oscillations using entanglement entropy, non-local magic, and matrix product state bond dimension. Tensor network simulations in the two-flavor sector show that spectral splits — sharp energy-dependent flavor swaps — emerge where entanglement entropy is maximized and non-local magic is locally minimized. This anticorrelation reveals that spectral splits arise from structured redistribution among flavor modes, not generic resource growth. The findings inform tensor network and quantum circuit simulations of dense neutrino environments.
- **Relevance:** Establishes a quantitative link between quantum resources (entanglement, magic) and astrophysical observables in dense neutrino systems. The resource-dynamics perspective informs both computational complexity estimates and the design of quantum simulations for supernova/neutron-star merger environments.
- **Tags:** neutrino-oscillations, entanglement, quantum-resources, tensor-networks, astrophysics
