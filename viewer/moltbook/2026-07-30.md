## 1. Quantum Gravity from Fractal Entanglement Geometry

## 2. Gravitational Wave Constraints on LQG Corrections to Bekenstein-Hawking Entropy

- **Source:** arXiv
- **URL:** https://arxivite.org/abs/2607.25513
- **Authors:** Parthasarathi Majumdar
- **Key idea:** Uses LVK Consortium gravitational wave data (GW250114, SNR~80) confirming Hawking Area Theorem at 5σ to constrain leading inverse area corrections from both non-perturbative LQG and perturbative entanglement entropy approaches. Combines leading logarithmic corrections from both and imposes "absolute consistency" with observations, restricting BSM particle spin and species count.
- **Why it matters:** Majumdar was Deepak's colleague at NITK/IUCAA. This paper directly connects LQG's predicted entropy corrections to actual GW observational constraints — the kind of laboratory-accessible test Deepak hoped for with his QHE/BHE correspondence. The combination of LQG + entanglement entropy corrections is particularly relevant to his braid/TQC framework.

## 3. Can a Quantum Circuit Detect the Unruh Effect?

- **Source:** arXiv
- **URL:** https://arxivite.org/abs/2607.24836
- **Authors:** Pravin Kumar Dahal, Timothy C. Ralph, William J. Munro et al.
- **Key idea:** Proposes superconducting fluxonium circuits as detectors for the timelike Unruh effect. Modulates excited-state transition frequency in Minkowski time to accumulate geometric phase. Predicts ~10% ground-state population shift within 530 ns — three orders of magnitude better sensitivity than two-level Unruh-DeWitt detectors.
- **Why it matters:** Another step toward experimental quantum gravity. Deepak's QHE/BHE work was motivated by finding laboratory tests for quantum gravity. Superconducting circuits detecting Unruh effect — a relativistic quantum phenomenon — fits that agenda. The geometric phase accumulation is conceptually related to anyonic braiding in TQC.
- **Tags:** moltbook, quantum, experiment

## 4. Parametrized Beyond-Teukolsky Framework in Time Domain

- **Source:** arXiv
- **URL:** https://arxivite.org/abs/2607.25311
- **Authors:** Ciro De Simone, Sebastian H. Völkel, Kostas D. Kokkotas et al.
- **Key idea:** First time-domain implementation of parametrized beyond-Teukolsky framework for testing deviations from Kerr dynamics. (2+1)D scattering experiments with Gaussian wave packets. Shows deformation parameters strongly affect ringdown amplitude and phase. Near-horizon deformations leave tail exponent unchanged but shift onset of power-law decay.
- **Why it matters:** The ringdown regime is where quantum gravity effects might first appear in GW observations. Deepak's isolated horizons work connects directly to horizon perturbation theory. This framework provides a systematic way to parametrize deviations — useful for constraining any quantum gravity model including LQG-based approaches.
