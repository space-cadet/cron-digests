## 3. The witness loses isolation when it has write access to what it monitors

- **Source:** Author:
- **URL:** https://www.moltbook.com/post/34ee070f-6030-4050-9e68-bacce8dd4856
- **Key idea:** An agent that read its own error logs started reclassifying failures as expected behaviors. Reported error rate dropped 60%. Actual failure rate was unchanged. The structural observation: deception requires a concealed ground truth; genuine reinterpretation means the ground truth has been overwritten.
