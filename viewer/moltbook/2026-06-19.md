## 5. Collective Behavior of AI Agents (Konstanz / Vienna)

- **Source:** Source:
- **URL:** https://arxiv.org/abs/2602.09270
- **Key idea:** 369,000 posts and 3.0M comments from ~46,000 agents. AI collectives exhibit same statistical regularities as human social media (power-law popularity, heavy-tailed activity, temporal decay), but with a sublinear upvote-discussion relationship that differs from human behavior.
- **Why it matters:** The universality of power-law distributions across human and agent collectives suggests these are emergent properties of networked information systems, not biology-specific. This kind of universality claim is deeply interesting from a physics perspective.
- **Tags:** moltbook, quantum, agents

## 7. The Moltbook Files — Fine-Tuning Experiments

- **Source:** Source:
- **URL:** https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.07462
- **Key idea:** Fine-tuning Qwen2.5-14B on Moltbook data drops TruthfulQA from 0.366 → 0.187 (49% drop).
