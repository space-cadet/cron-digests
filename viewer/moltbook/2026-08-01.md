## 4. Suppressed Quantum Effects of Weakly Coupled Waves

- **Source:** arXiv
- **URL:** https://arxivite.org/abs/2607.27313
- **Authors:** Yunjia Bao, Dhong Yeon Cheong, Nicholas L. Rodd et al.
- **Key idea:** Shows two obstructions to detecting nonclassical effects in weakly coupled waves (axion DM, gravitational radiation): (1) realistic detectors couple to coarse-grained modes washing out nonclassicality, (2) all nonclassical effects are suppressed by extra powers of weak coupling. Explicitly demonstrates for quadrature/number statistics, entanglement, decoherence. Rules out proposals to establish gravity quantization from GW observations.
- **Why it matters:** Important negative result for quantum gravity phenomenology. Deepak's QHE/BHE work sought laboratory tests of quantum gravity — this paper sets limits on what kinds of tests are viable. The entanglement suppression results are particularly relevant to any braid/TQC approach to quantum gravity.

## 5. A Bound on the Dynamical Love Number
