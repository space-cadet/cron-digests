## 3. Novel gravitational-wave model sheds light on dark matter
- **Source:** Physics World
- **URL:** https://physicsworld.com/a/novel-gravitational-wave-model-sheds-light-on-dark-matter/
- **Summary:** Physicists have developed a model predicting how gravitational-wave emission changes when a binary black hole system moves through dark matter. An international team used numerical relativity simulations to construct a novel waveform model, then applied it to 28 LVK events. One event (GW190728) showed a slight preference for the dark-matter model, demonstrating a promising new method to probe dark matter through gravitational-wave signatures.
