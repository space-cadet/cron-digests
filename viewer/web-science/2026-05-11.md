## 7. 'Dark Proteins' Reclassified as 'Peptideins' and Added to Databases
- **Source:** Nature News
- **URL:** https://www.nature.com/articles/d41586-026-01492-x
- **Summary:** Thousands of previously excluded "dark proteins" — short proteins with unknown functions — have been officially named "peptideins" and added to major gene and protein databases by the TransCODE Consortium. These molecules, encoded by the human genome, had been excluded from catalogs due to their short length and lack of evolutionary relatives.
- **Relevance:** Some peptideins have been linked to childhood cancers and basic cellular functions. Their formal recognition could spark a new wave of research into previously overlooked biological mechanisms and potential therapeutic targets.
