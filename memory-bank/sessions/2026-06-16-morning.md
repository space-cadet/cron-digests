### Fix 2: Category Extraction Regex
- **File:** `scripts/fetch-arxiv-html.py`
- **Change:** Extract category codes from within parentheses `(hep-th)`, with fallback for bare codes.
- **Pattern:** `\(([a-zA-Z\-]+(?:\.[a-zA-Z\-]+)?)\)`

### Fix 3: Retroactive Repair
