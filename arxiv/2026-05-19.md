## 1. Emergent Thiemann Coherent States in Quantum Reduced Loop Gravity
- **Authors:** Ilkka Mäkinen, Hanno Sahlmann, Waleed Sherif
- **arXiv ID:** 2605.18625
- **URL:** https://arxivite.org/abs/2605.18625
- **Categories:** gr-qc, hep-th, physics.comp-ph
- **Summary:** Variational Monte Carlo analysis of the near-kernel sector of the Hamiltonian constraint in the one-vertex model of quantum reduced loop gravity, using neural quantum states. Near-kernel states organize into three classes: non-factorized at low cutoffs, and two factorized branches at larger cutoffs. One branch matches reduced Thiemann coherent states with near-unit fidelity, providing evidence for emergent semiclassical organization.
- **Relevance:** Direct numerical evidence that semiclassical coherent states emerge naturally from the quantum dynamics of loop quantum gravity. The neural-quantum-state methodology is novel for LQG and could be extended to more complex models.
- **Tags:** Loop Quantum Gravity, Quantum Cosmology, Neural Quantum States, Coherent States, Semiclassical Limit

## 2. Quasinormal Modes of a Rotating Loop Quantum Black Hole
- **Authors:** Zhongzhinan Dong, Shulan Li, Dan Zhang, Jian-Pin Wu
- **arXiv ID:** 2605.18249
- **URL:** https://arxivite.org/abs/2605.18249
- **Categories:** gr-qc
- **Summary:** Investigation of quasinormal modes of a massless scalar field on an effective rotating loop quantum black hole background, constructed via an improved Newman-Janis algorithm. Quantum gravity corrections monotonically reduce both oscillation frequency and damping rate. Rotation imprints nontrivial modulation: real frequency displays a crossover for spherically symmetric perturbations as spin grows, while angular momentum suppresses this feature. Overtone sector retains hallmark quantum gravitational signatures including outbursts.
- **Relevance:** First systematic study of rotating LQBH quasinormal modes. The quantum correction signatures in the overtone sector are observationally relevant for gravitational-wave ringdown physics and could discriminate quantum-corrected black holes from classical Kerr.
- **Tags:** Loop Quantum Black Holes, Quasinormal Modes, Gravitational Waves, Quantum Gravity Phenomenology

## 3. The Problem of Time: A Path Integral View
- **Authors:** Juan Manuel Diaz, Alejandro Perez
- **arXiv ID:** 2605.17935
- **URL:** https://arxivite.org/abs/2605.17935
- **Categories:** gr-qc, hep-th, quant-ph
- **Summary:** Schrödinger evolution is recovered in a timeless nonrelativistic closed quantum system (a proxy for generally covariant theories) via the path integral once a clock degree of freedom is placed in a semiclassical "good-clock" state. The analysis shows that the "cosine problem" (transition amplitudes taking the form exp(iS/ℏ)+exp(−iS/ℏ)) is a natural consequence of time-reversal symmetry in the path integral, not a defect requiring modification.
- **Relevance:** Perez is a central figure in LQG. The paper offers an intuitive path-integral resolution of the cosine problem that has troubled spin-foam formulations, suggesting backward propagation is physically meaningful rather than an artifact to eliminate.
- **Tags:** Problem of Time, Path Integral, Spin Foam, Loop Quantum Gravity, Quantum Cosmology

## 4. Scalar-Tensor Gravity as a Probe of Generalized Black Hole Entropy
- **Authors:** Hussain Gohar
- **arXiv ID:** 2605.18551
- **URL:** https://arxivite.org/abs/2605.18551
- **Categories:** gr-qc, hep-th, quant-ph
- **Summary:** A geometric realization connecting generalized black hole entropy functionals (Barrow, Tsallis-Cirto, quantum-gravity corrections) to the Misner-Sharp quasilocal mass and Wald Noether-charge entropy in scalar-tensor theories. Distinct scalar potentials emerge: exponential for Barrow, exponential-with-parameter for Tsallis-Cirto, approximately linear for quantum corrections. Compatible with solar-system, BBN, and pulsar-timing constraints.
- **Relevance:** Unifies information-theoretic entropy proposals with gravitational field theory through a scalar-tensor bridge. The explicit potentials and their cosmological phenomenology (inflation, late-time dark energy, bouncing cosmologies) are testable.
- **Tags:** Black Hole Thermodynamics, Generalized Entropy, Scalar-Tensor Gravity, Quantum Gravity Corrections, Cosmology
