## 1. Variational Quantization of Gravitational Fields via Relative Entropy
- **Authors:** Jianhao M. Yang
- **arXiv ID:** 2605.05239
- **URL:** https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.05239
- **Categories:** quant-ph (cross-list hep-th)
- **Relevance:** 🔥 High — Develops a variational framework for quantizing gravitational fields without the usual operator-ordering ambiguity in canonical quantization. Recovers the Wheeler-DeWitt equation from a generalized stationary action principle incorporating relative entropy. When coupled to a scalar field, recovers a Schrödinger equation with quantum corrections at order $G\hbar^2$. Explicitly connects to holographic dualities. A fresh foundational take on quantum gravity and quantum cosmology.

## 4. No-Boundary Wavefunction on the 3-Torus Favors Large Universe
- **Authors:** Victor Godet et al.
- **arXiv ID:** 2605.05317
- **URL:** https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.05317
- **Categories:** hep-th (cross-list gr-qc)
- **Relevance:** High — Resolves the long-standing problem that Hartle–Hawking no-boundary wavefunctions on $S^3$ exponentially favor small universes. By changing topology to $T^3$ and summing over SL(3,ℤ) geometries using automorphic forms for GL(3), the wavefunction now favors large inflating universes with $\mathcal{N} \gtrsim 250$ e-folds. Also computes corrections to the CMB power spectrum from torus moduli fluctuations. A significant advance in quantum cosmology.

## 7. Biplanar SPOQC Architecture for Fermi-Hubbard Simulation
- **Authors:** Boris Bourdoncle, Peter-Jan Derks, Théo Dessertaine, Johannes Frank
- **arXiv ID:** 2605.05315
- **URL:** https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.05315
- **Categories:** quant-ph
- **Relevance:** High — Estimates costs for simulating the 2D Fermi-Hubbard model on a biplanar spin-optical quantum computing (SPOQC) architecture using honeycomb Floquet code. Eliminates fermionic swap operations and reduces Trotter step depth from $6t_{\text{synth}} + 354$ to $4t_{\text{synth}} + 90$ logical timesteps. For $L=8$, $U/t=8$, estimates ~2 hour runtime with $1.35 \times 10^6$ physical qubits. Identifies rotation synthesis as a scalability bottleneck. Concrete architectural co-design for near-term quantum simulation.

## 8. Quantum Resolution of Big Bang Singularity via Page–Wootters
- **Authors:** Malay K. Nandy et al.
- **arXiv ID:** 2605.06093
- **URL:** https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.06093
- **Categories:** gr-qc
- **Relevance:** High — Investigates the classical big bang singularity in Bianchi type-I within the Wheeler–DeWitt framework, using the Page–Wootters formalism to address the problem of time. Constructs Gaussian superpositions in Misner variables; the conditional probability density vanishes at zero volume for all clock values, indicating quantum singularity resolution. Positivity constraints on the probability density impose admissible clock values depending on wavepacket parameters. A clean demonstration of relational quantum dynamics resolving cosmological singularities.

## 9. Navier–Stokes Hydrodynamics from Chaotic XXZ Spin Chain via gEDMD
- **Authors:** Seiki Saito et al.
- **arXiv ID:** 2605.05604
- **URL:** https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.05604
- **Categories:** quant-ph (cross-list from cond-mat)
- **Relevance:** Medium-High — Introduces a fully data-driven framework combining generalized Extended Dynamic Mode Decomposition (gEDMD) with Mori–Zwanzig projection to extract Navier–Stokes hydrodynamic coefficients from a chaotic XXZ spin chain. Reveals that macroscopic friction and viscosity are not absolute properties but emergent phenomena requiring finite temporal coarse-graining. Mechanical elasticity is derived from exact unitary dynamics, while friction only emerges at observer timescales. Connects to fundamental questions about irreversibility in isolated quantum systems.
