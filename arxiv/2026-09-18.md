## 1. Supersymmetric boundary algebras in AdS$_3$ gravity: constraints, Dirac brackets and non-locality
**Authors:** Nabamita Banerjee, Vedant Bhutra, Suvankar Dutta, Soumava Kundu  
**URL:** https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.20112v1  
**Categories:** hep-th  
- **Abstract:*** We study supersymmetric boundary dynamics in AdS$_3$ gravity by treating boundary conditions as constrained reductions of a generic Chern-Simons boundary phase space. We focus on how the constraint structure controls the locality and physical field content of the reduced asymptotic symmetry algebra. For supersymmetric Kac--Moody boundary conditions, the surviving bosonic currents remain local, whereas the fermion--fermion Dirac bracket contains the inverse of a field-dependent first-order differential operator and is therefore intrinsically non-local. Imposing an additional fermionic constraint reveals a further structure: after the bosonic second-class reduction, it becomes first class and generates a fermionic gauge redundancy. Generically the remaining fermionic field can be gauged away, while special backgrounds with periodic zero modes support non-trivial physical fermionic sectors. We further employ the Batalin--Fradkin--Tyutin formalism to eliminate the non-locality of the reduced symmetry algebra by converting the second-class constraints into an equivalent local first-class system on an enlarged phase space.

- **Summary:*** The authors reduce supersymmetric AdS$_3$ Chern–Simons boundary dynamics using Dirac’s constrained-Hamiltonian machinery, then use Batalin–Fradkin–Tyutin conversion to localize the resulting algebra. They find bosonic currents stay local but fermionic brackets are generically non-local, with gauge-removable fermions except for periodic zero modes; the enlarged local formulation clarifies the physical boundary sectors.

**RELEVANCE:** Directly relevant to quantum gravity, holography, and the canonical treatment of boundary degrees of freedom.
**TAGS:** `AdS3` `boundary symmetries` `constrained quantization`

## 2. When Is Kramers-Wannier Duality Invertible?
**Authors:** Akash Sinha, Pramod Padmanabhan, Vladimir Korepin  
**URL:** https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.20090v1  
**Categories:** cond-mat.str-el, cond-mat.stat-mech, hep-th  
- **Abstract:*** *Kramers-Wannier* duality of the quantum Ising chain is naturally realized as a noninvertible transformation on a finite periodic chain. Upon incorporating appropriate symmetry-twisted sectors, however, the duality can be promoted to an invertible unitary transformation. In this Letter, we classify when the Kramers-Wannier duality admits an invertible realization on a finite Hilbert space. We show that this is determined by the representation of the Ising bond algebra and establish a necessary and sufficient condition in terms of its two central elements. This representation-theoretic criterion unifies the conventional noninvertible realization with invertible constructions involving symmetry-twisted sectors and applies equally to other models with the same underlying bond algebra. As an explicit example, we find an order-disorder duality in a non-trivial realization of the Ising bond algebra that admits an invertible Kramers-Wannier duality.

- **Summary:*** A representation-theoretic analysis of the Ising bond algebra yields a necessary-and-sufficient criterion for turning finite-chain Kramers–Wannier duality into a unitary map. Twisted sectors supply the missing invertibility, and an explicit nontrivial representation demonstrates the construction, connecting lattice duality to modern non-invertible symmetry ideas.

**RELEVANCE:** Useful for links among condensed matter, generalized symmetries, and quantum-information descriptions of dualities.
**TAGS:** `duality` `non-invertible symmetry` `Ising model`

## 3. Statistical delocalization in the Anyonic Aubry-André model
**Authors:** Andoni Agirre, André Eckardt, Tobias Grass  
**URL:** https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.20029v1  
**Categories:** cond-mat.quant-gas, quant-ph  
- **Abstract:*** Many-body localization is remarkable in that localization persists despite the presence of dynamical interactions. Here we demonstrate the converse phenomenon: enhanced many-body delocalization induced solely by exchange statistics. We study a Aubry-André quasiperiodic system of anyons in one dimension in the absence of density-density interactions. In terms of anyon operators, the system is described by a quadratic Hamiltonian. By analyzing the finite-size scaling of its spectral properties and the inverse participation ratio of many-body eigenstates, we find that increasing the anyonic statistical phase systematically shifts the many-body localization crossover toward larger quasiperdiodic potentials. We further confirm these findings using a dynamical probe, namely the persistence of an initial density imbalance following quenches. Our results establish exchange statistics as an independent mechanism capable of altering many-body localization, revealing a fundamentally distinct route to delocalization in one-dimensional quantum systems.

- **Summary:*** Exact quadratic anyon dynamics are combined with finite-size spectral scaling, inverse participation ratios, and quench imbalance measurements. Increasing the statistical angle moves the localization crossover to stronger quasiperiodic disorder, showing that exchange statistics alone can drive delocalization without density interactions.

**RELEVANCE:** A sharp many-body-physics result connecting anyons, localization, and experimentally accessible quench diagnostics.
**TAGS:** `anyons` `many-body localization` `quasiperiodic systems`

## 4. Corner entanglement scaling with projected entangled pair states
**Authors:** Chloé Van Bastelaere, Rui-Zhen Huang, Laurens Vanderstraeten  
**URL:** https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.20020v1  
**Categories:** cond-mat.str-el, quant-ph  
- **Abstract:*** Entanglement scaling provides a powerful probe of universal properties at quantum critical points. In two dimensions, contributions originating from a corner-shaped bipartition exhibit a universal scaling, which is determined by the underlying conformal field theory. We develop a method to extract this corner entanglement entropy from projected entangled pair states directly in the thermodynamic limit. When applied to models at a quantum critical point, we show that the corner contribution exhibits scaling with the effective correlation length, in agreement with the hypothesis of finite-entanglement scaling. Our results for the corner coefficients are consistent with other methods, demonstrating the efficiency of our method for diagnosing strongly-correlated quantum critical points in two dimensions.

- **Summary:*** The work develops a thermodynamic-limit PEPS procedure for extracting universal corner entanglement. Its finite-entanglement-scaling analysis reproduces known corner coefficients at criticality, providing a practical numerical diagnostic for two-dimensional strongly correlated systems.

**RELEVANCE:** Relevant to tensor networks, entanglement diagnostics, and quantum critical matter.
**TAGS:** `PEPS` `entanglement entropy` `quantum criticality`

## 5. Distinguishing Coupled Dark Matter Dark Energy from Kinematic Phantom Crossing
**Authors:** Samit Ganguly, Koushik Dutta, Goutam Manna  
**URL:** https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.19602v1  
**Categories:** astro-ph.CO, gr-qc  
- **Abstract:*** Recent measurements of cosmic expansion have renewed interest in dark-energy scenarios in which the effective equation of state exhibits phantom-like evolution and may cross $w=-1$. Such behavior, however, does not require a fundamental phantom field: in coupled dark-energy-dark-matter~(CDEDM) models, energy exchange between a canonical scalar field and cold dark matter, happens Lagrangian labels, can reproduce the same effective equation of state evolution as that of phenomenological $w_0w_a$CDM model. We show that this microscopic interaction leaves distinctive, testable signatures in structure growth by forming a two-fluid system in which only cold dark matter feels the scalar-mediated fifth force (and an associated drag term), while baryons remain uncoupled. When combined with neutrino-free streaming, this setup generates a characteristic coupling, neutrino-mass degeneracy, whose leading scaling $\sum m_ν\propto f_c^2 α^2$ follows analytically from the two-fluid growth equations. Using DESI DR2 BAO, CMB distance information, ACT DR6 lensing, redshift-space distortions, and three supernova compilations, we find that this scaling is recovered for both exponential and inverse-power-law potentials. We further show that the dominant contribution to the growth response arises from the coupling-induced modification of the background evolution, with the fifth force enhancing growth and the drag partially counteracting it. Finally, the interaction induces a running dark-matter mass, changing it at recombination by $4.3\%$--$5.5\%$, so CMB distance calculations must account for the evolving dark-matter density. These correlated growth and recombination effects provide a route to distinguish interaction-driven phantom crossing from purely kinematic dark-energy parametrizations.

- **Summary:*** A two-fluid perturbation analysis plus DESI, CMB, lensing, RSD, and supernova data separates interacting dark-sector phantom crossing from phenomenological $w(z)$. The predicted coupling–neutrino-mass degeneracy and 4.3–5.5% recombination-era dark-matter mass change identify growth and distance observables that can test the microscopic model.

**RELEVANCE:** High relevance to cosmology and modified dark-sector dynamics, with direct observational handles.
**TAGS:** `dark energy` `structure growth` `DESI`

## 6. Relativistic Stellar Oscillations from Ultralight Dark Matter
**Authors:** Shanae Oishi, Christopher Reyes, Jeremy Sakstein  
**URL:** https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.19500v1  
**Categories:** gr-qc, astro-ph.CO, astro-ph.HE, hep-ph  
- **Abstract:*** Ultra-light dark matter, composed of bosons behaving as classical waves, can resonantly excite stellar oscillations. In this work, we investigate this phenomenon in relativistic stars, showing that $\ell=0$ fluid modes can be excited. We derive a framework for calculating the mode amplitude and apply it to neutron stars to predict the associated surface temperature fluctuations; the resulting signal lies beyond current observational sensitivities. We discuss prospects for extending this approach to other compact objects.

- **Summary:*** The authors derive a relativistic mode-driving framework for wave-like ultralight dark matter and show that radial ($\ell=0$) stellar modes can resonate in compact stars. Applying it to neutron stars predicts temperature modulations currently too small to observe, but establishes a calculable route to probe ultralight fields with other compact objects.

**RELEVANCE:** Connects dark-matter phenomenology with relativistic stellar physics and potential precision tests of gravity.
**TAGS:** `ultralight dark matter` `neutron stars` `stellar oscillations`

## 7. A Global Overview of Starobinsky Inflation beyond slow-roll with CMB-BAO data
**Authors:** Tarun, Tanmoy Modak, Björn Malte Schäfer  
**URL:** https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.19296v1  
**Categories:** astro-ph.CO, gr-qc, hep-ph  
- **Abstract:*** We perform a global analysis of the Starobinsky inflation using most recent CMB data from ACT and SPT together with DESI BAO measurements. By directly solving the equations of motion, we find relatively stronger constraints on the model parameters than those obtained under the slow-roll approximation from the baseline $Λ$CDM parameters $A_s$ and $n_s$. We translate these constraints into limits on the reheating temperature. We also discuss the dependence of the prior choices of the model parameters. Further, the correlation of the model parameters with the BAO parameter $r_d h$ is also studied. We further investigate the impact of the global analysis on the dimension-six $R^3$ modified Starobinsky model, deriving constraints on its additional parameters. Finally, we also provide parameter limits on the effective inflationary Hubble slow-roll (HSR) and potential slow-roll (PSR) parametrization.

- **Summary:*** Instead of imposing slow roll, this study numerically solves the inflationary equations while fitting ACT/SPT CMB and DESI BAO data. The resulting tighter parameter bounds propagate to reheating limits and the dimension-six $R^3$ extension, while prior and $r_dh$ correlations quantify key analysis systematics.

**RELEVANCE:** Timely constraints on inflation and higher-curvature corrections relevant to quantum-gravity-inspired cosmology.
**TAGS:** `Starobinsky inflation` `CMB` `reheating`

## 8. Non-Abelian Anyon Condensation: A Path-Integral Monte Carlo Approach
**Authors:** Rafael Flores-Calderón, Frank Pollmann, Michael Knap  
**URL:** https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.19282v1  
**Categories:** cond-mat.str-el, hep-lat, quant-ph  
- **Abstract:*** Transitions out of non-Abelian topological order are difficult to describe in microscopic quantum models with numerical methods that remain tractable at large scales. We develop a sign-free path-integral framework for Kitaev quantum doubles $\mathcal D(G)$ by organizing single-link perturbations in terms of non-invertible electric and magnetic 1-form symmetries that proliferate distinct anyon species. For any finite group $G$, an exact $\textit{matterization}$ isometry introduces vertex degrees of freedom and maps the link-only model onto a $G$ gauge--Higgs theory. Furthermore, the 1-form symmetries of the fixed point allow us to define generalized Fredenhagen--Marcu order parameters that become finite when the corresponding anyons condense. For $G = S_3$, quantum Monte Carlo simulations show that proliferating a non-Abelian electric anyon drives a first-order transition in which all nontrivial electric anyons condense. In the purely magnetic limit, the model reduces to a $(2+1)$D pure $G$ gauge theory; for $G=S_3$, it exhibits a first-order confinement transition, diagnosed by the onset of a Wilson-loop area law and the restoration of an emergent magnetic 1-form symmetry. These results provide a unified numerical framework for non-Abelian anyon condensation, confinement, and generalized symmetry breaking.

- **Summary:*** A sign-free path-integral Monte Carlo formulation, enabled by matterization and non-invertible 1-form symmetry variables, makes large-scale studies of non-Abelian condensation feasible. For $S_3$, simulations find first-order electric-condensation and magnetic-confinement transitions, diagnosed by generalized order parameters and Wilson-loop scaling.

**RELEVANCE:** Particularly valuable at the intersection of topological matter, lattice gauge theory, and quantum information.
**TAGS:** `non-Abelian anyons` `quantum Monte Carlo` `lattice gauge theory`

## 9. Topological entanglement entropy in 3D gravity
**Authors:** Vijay Balasubramanian, Charlie Cummings  
**URL:** https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.19247v1  
**Categories:** hep-th, cond-mat.str-el, gr-qc, quant-ph  
- **Abstract:*** We derive an entropy formula for a recently proposed tensor network model of states in 3D gravity which satisfy the constraint equations of diffeomorphism invariance, and including the effects of matter. After accounting for the invertibility of the spacetime metric, we argue that this formula matches the quantum extremal surface formula (for small but finite $G_N$), and is not restricted to time symmetric spacetimes. We derive the result via canonical quantization without relying on path integrals, and discuss an extension to compact bulk subregions.

- **Summary:*** Canonical quantization of a diffeomorphism-constrained tensor-network model produces an entropy formula including matter and metric invertibility. It agrees with the quantum-extremal-surface prescription at small finite $G_N$ and extends beyond time-symmetric slices, strengthening the gravity–entanglement bridge.

**RELEVANCE:** Directly aligned with quantum gravity, holography, tensor networks, and entanglement entropy.

## 10. 90%-Efficient Optical Storage in a Rare-Earth Crystal with a Low-Loss Impedance-Matched Cavity
**Authors:** Weiye Sun, Fudong Wang, Mucheng Guo, Xiantong An, Zhen Xu, Zhehao Xu, Xingmin He, Matthew J. Sellars, Shuping Liu, Manjin Zhong  
**URL:** https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.19807v1  
**Categories:** quant-ph  
- **Abstract:*** High-efficiency optical storage in rare-earth-ion-doped crystals is challenging because of their typically weak optical absorption,which makes cavity-enhanced memories highly sensitive to parasitic intracavity loss. Here we demonstrate a 90.1(5)% storage efficiency for coherent optical pulses in an impedance-matched cavity atomic-frequency-comb memory based on Eu3+:Y2SiO5. The Brewster-angle configuration of the crystal and fully cryogenic Fabry-Pérot cavity reduce the round-trip intracavity loss to 0.37%. Quantitative modeling shows that further improvement is limited primarily by AFC preparation and its associated dispersive response rather than by residual cavity loss,identifying the principal requirements for further approaching unity efficiency.

- **Summary:*** An impedance-matched cryogenic Fabry–Pérot cavity and Brewster-angle Eu$^{3+}$:Y$_2$SiO$_5$ memory achieve 90.1(5)% coherent-pulse storage, with only 0.37% round-trip loss. Modeling identifies atomic-frequency-comb preparation and dispersion—not cavity loss—as the remaining bottleneck toward unity efficiency.

**RELEVANCE:** A strong experimental advance for quantum memories and scalable photonic quantum networking.
**TAGS:** `quantum memory` `rare-earth crystals` `photonic networking`
