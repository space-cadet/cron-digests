## hep-th

**2605.15258** — *Generating Rotation in a Snap* (Chakraborty, Heidmann, Patashuri)  
Algebraic technique to generate rotating black-hole solutions from static ones via AdS×S asymptotics — no need to solve Einstein equations. Recovers Kerr and Myers–Perry directly from Schwarzschild.

**2605.15269** — *Kerroll black holes* (Ecker, Grumiller, Hörl, Leturcq–Daligaux, Pérez)  
Rotating solutions in Carroll gravity. Two routes: (i) intrinsically Carrollian rotation by dressing the Carroll-Schwarzschild solution, and (ii) an odd-power expansion in *c* from GR that yields a Carroll analog of Kerr — the "Kerroll" black hole.

**2605.15272** — *A general proof of integer Rényi QNEC* (Kibe, Roy)  
Proves the Rényi quantum null energy condition for all integer *n* ≥ 2 using von Neumann algebras with half-sided modular inclusion. Shows log-convexity of the Kosaki *L*ⁿ norm under null-translation semigroups.

**2605.15293** — *Dimensional reduction of AdS₃ Chern-Simons gravity* (Chirco, Vacchiano, Vitale)  
Symmetry-reduced AdS₃/ℤ₂ gravity → 2D BF-like model + 1D boundary action. Two sectors: (a) standard Schwarzian (Drinfel’d–Sokolov reduction, JT gravity), and (b) deformed Schwarzian with affine residual symmetry tied to a non-extremal / Rindler-type regime.

**2605.15968** — *Anomalous Transport from Effective Field Theory* (Larue, Marchon, Quevillon, Saviot)  
Integrates out a massive Dirac fermion at finite *T* in vector + axial background fields. Unified derivation of chiral magnetic / separation effects with new mass corrections; clarifies that anomalous transport persists even when the anomaly itself vanishes locally.

## gr-qc

**2605.15251** — *Quantum Gravity Beyond the Bulk* (Gamboa, Tapia-Arellano) ⭐  
Infrared / asymptotic formulation for external observers. In a proper-time gauge the evolution generator reduces to the Regge–Teitelboim boundary charge. Born–Oppenheimer separation between slow asymptotic data and fast bulk fluctuations; a Berry connection on admissible configurations governs infrared-dressed gravitational states. Entropy of the reduced density matrix is dominated by global infrared holonomy sectors rather than local bulk dynamics. *(Gravity Research Foundation Honorable Mention)*

**2605.15255** — *Dymnikova Black Holes in Unimodular Gravity* (Alencar, Borralho)  
Regular black-hole solution within unimodular gravity. Controlled violation of covariant ∇ᵤTᵘᵛ = 0 gives a radial-dependent Λ(*r*). Same geometry generated by both nonlinear and standard Maxwell electrodynamics; electric field is everywhere regular with vanishing asymptotic charge.

**2605.15858** — *Loop Quantum Kaluza-Klein Cosmology and Inflation* (Li, Ma, Yuan, Zhang) ⭐  
Five-dimensional loop-quantum Kaluza-Klein cosmology from symmetric reduction of the connection formulation. Effective scalar constraint with quantum fluctuations as subleading correction. Big-bang and big-rip singularities resolved by leading-order holonomy corrections. Achieves ~55 e-folds of super-inflation after the bounce; inclusion of the fluctuation term produces re-collapse at large scales.

## quant-ph

**2605.15247** — *Quantum Meets Statistical-Physical Secrecy* (Basar)  
Hybrid QKD + KLJN (Kirchhoff-law-Johnson-noise) architecture. Three protocols that eliminate public basis disclosure, remove bit sifting, or generate raw key bits under ideal KLJN conditions. Analytical key-rate expressions accounting for optical penalties and KLJN bandwidth limits.

**2605.15266** — *Synthesis and Optimization of Encoding Circuits for FTQC* (Peham, Steinberg, Wille, Heußen)  
Encoder synthesis formulated as search over stabilizer tableaus. Greedy and rollout-based algorithms exploit stabilizer-equivalent realizations. Improvements up to **43 %** in two-qubit gate count and **70 %** in depth vs. literature, evaluated on holographic and qLDPC codes.

**2605.15277** — *Beyond Unitary Quantum Simulation* (Marthaler, Zapusek, Reiter)  
Review arguing that open-system dynamics (dissipation, thermalization, decoherence) is a more realistic route to quantum advantage in chemistry than pure ground-state unitary evolution. Discusses exponential advantage cases and what remains unknown.

**2605.15297** — *Towards Deploying Optimistic Quantum Fourier Transforms* (Lopes)  
Architecture-algorithm co-design for the Optimistic QFT under surface-code FT on reconfigurable neutral-atom hardware. Hot-zone architecture decouples storage from processing. Tunable parallelism / latency trade-off: 4 hot zones ≈ halve runtime; additional zones approach constant-time at substantial resource cost.

**2605.15366** — *Measurement-Efficient VQLS for Carleman-Linearized Nonlinear Dynamics* (Liu, Wang)  
Solves the Duffing equation via Carleman linearization + Variational Quantum Linear Solver. Symmetry-grouped Hadamard Test evaluations under global and local cost functions. Near-unity fidelity and vanishing residuals on IBM and Xanadu platforms.

## cond-mat

**2605.15274** — *Unveiling Magnetic Frustration via the Elastocaloric Effect* (Andrade, Cônsoli, Vojta)  
Ising and Heisenberg models on anisotropic triangular (and kagome) lattices. The elastic Grüneisen ratio *η* diverges at low *T* near maximal frustration — universal signature of an extensive ground-state entropy.

**2605.15332** — *Disorder-driven symmetry suppression in a magnetic topological insulator* (Joris et al.)  
Ion irradiation of MnBi₂Te₄ introduces van der Waals planar defects. A new layer-disordered phase suppresses the anomalous Hall response by >1 order of magnitude, far exceeding magnetization changes — indicating direct modification of Berry curvature.

**2605.15447** — *Multifractal and Ergodic Properties of Conductance Fluctuations under Strong Disorder* (de Sousa et al.)  
2D tight-binding Anderson model. Conductance fluctuations transition from non-ergodic to ergodic as disorder increases; multifractality persists in both regimes but becomes shuffle-insensitive in the strong-disorder (ergodic) limit.

**2605.15610** — *Fractal analysis of disordered magnetic systems* (Hermann et al.)  
Edwards-Anderson Hamiltonian. Monte Carlo validation showing disorder alters critical exponents (new universality classes) while a subgroup of exponents and fractal dimensions remains invariant — heralding a "strong universality class."
