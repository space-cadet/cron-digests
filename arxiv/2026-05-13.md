## 1. Euclidean Wineglass Wormholes and No-Boundary Instantons
- **Authors:** Jean-Luc Lehners, et al.
- **arXiv ID:** 2605.10548
- **URL:** https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.10548
- **Categories:** hep-th, gr-qc
- **Summary:** Studies Euclidean wineglass wormholes that mediate nucleation of inflationary spacetimes from asymptotically flat or AdS regions. These wormholes feature a local maximum of the scale factor, enabling the Lorentzian continuation to expand after materialization. Solutions supported by axionic or magnetic gauge fields are presented, alongside exotic multi-extrema geometries. In the small-charge limit, wineglass solutions split into a background spacetime and a disconnected no-boundary instanton, revealing a topology-changing transition.
- **Relevance:** Directly addresses quantum cosmology and the origin of spacetime geometry. Connects wormhole physics to the no-boundary proposal, highly relevant to quantum gravity research.
- **Tags:** Quantum Gravity, Cosmology & Early Universe

## 2. Quantum Trajectory Simulation of 2D Non-Equilibrium Steady States with a Trapped Ion Quantum Processor
- **Authors:** Anna Dalmasso, Arash Jafarizadeh, Julian Boesl, et al.
- **arXiv ID:** 2605.08350
- **URL:** https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.08350
- **Categories:** quant-ph, cond-mat.stat-mech, cond-mat.str-el
- **Summary:** Uses the Quantinuum H1 trapped-ion quantum computer to realize quantum trajectories for hard-core bosons/fermions on a 2D square lattice with stochastic driving at source and drain corners. The non-equilibrium steady state exhibits persistent currents, with measurable effects from particle statistics, interactions, and magnetic fields. Demonstrates current capabilities and limitations of quantum hardware for many-body physics.
- **Relevance:** Experimental quantum computing applied to condensed matter physics — exactly the intersection of quantum computing and many-body systems of interest.
- **Tags:** Quantum Information & Computing, Many-Body Physics & Condensed Matter, Experiment & Observation

## 3. Gravielectric and Gravimagnetic Fluxes in Nutty Black Holes
- **Authors:** Dmitri Gal'tsov, Rostom Karsanov
- **arXiv ID:** 2605.09786
- **URL:** https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.09786
- **Categories:** gr-qc, hep-th
- **Summary:** Introduces gravielectric and gravimagnetic fields in stationary spacetime via the Komar two-form and its dual. Shows that Misner strings carry singular fluxes connecting horizon and asymptotic zone, with lateral transparency enabling field line flow. Explains why Komar mass integrals around Misner strings are negative in Taub-NUT vacuum, and demonstrates closed circuits without sources. Provides new perspective on mass and angular momentum in spacetime with NUT charge.
- **Relevance:** Novel approach to black hole mass and angular momentum definitions, with implications for quantum gravity treatments of black hole thermodynamics.
- **Tags:** Black Hole Physics, Quantum Gravity, Field Theory & Mathematical Physics

## 4. DART-Q: A Deadline-Driven Framework for Real-Time QLDPC Decoding
- **Authors:** Ameya S. Bhave, Navnil Choudhury, Kanad Basu
- **arXiv ID:** 2605.09142
- **URL:** https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.09142
- **Categories:** quant-ph
- **Summary:** Presents a real-time quantum error correction framework for quantum LDPC codes that treats decoding as deadline-driven queueing under strict timing and memory constraints. Key results: cached-summary state organization lowers SRAM-fit boundary by 4×, doubling decoder capacity reduces MissRate from 97.64% to 0.98%, and improves p99 latency from 3.861ms to 10μs. Exposes regime changes that determine real-time decoder viability.
- **Relevance:** Critical engineering work for fault-tolerant quantum computing. Real-time QLDPC decoding is essential for practical quantum computers.
- **Tags:** Quantum Information & Computing, Technology & Engineering

## 5. Analytical Solution of Traversable Wormholes with Positive Cosmological Constant
- **Authors:** Rajesh Karmakar, Xian-Hui Ge
- **arXiv ID:** 2605.08205
- **URL:** https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.08205
- **Categories:** gr-qc, hep-th
- **Summary:** Obtains an analytical traversable wormhole solution for Λ > 0 using gravitational decoupling, based on Ellis-Bronnikov geometry. The cosmological constant induces a second throat in addition to the standard wormhole throat, with modified asymptotic behavior (not asymptotically de Sitter). Verifies flare-out conditions and null energy condition violation at both throats. Derives constraints on safe human passage velocities through tidal force analysis.
- **Relevance:** Construction of exact wormhole solutions with cosmological constant bridges classical general relativity and quantum gravity phenomenology.
- **Tags:** Quantum Gravity, Black Hole Physics

## 7. Quantum Circuits with Quantum Control of Causal Order from Physical Principles
- **Authors:** Matthias Salzger, et al.
- **arXiv ID:** 2605.08351
- **URL:** https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.08351
- **Categories:** quant-ph
- **Summary:** Provides a rigorous top-down derivation of quantum circuits with quantum control of causal order (QC-QC) from relativistic causality principles. Uses causal boxes framework with Acting Once + Local Order constraints to prove that any protocol in classical acyclic spacetime satisfying these is behaviorally equivalent to a QC-QC. Shows QC-QCs constitute precisely the physically realizable class of higher-order quantum processes with indefinite causal order, ruling out more general non-causal processes under closed-labs assumptions.
- **Relevance:** Foundational result connecting abstract process matrix frameworks to experimentally accessible quantum protocols. Important for quantum gravity approaches involving indefinite causal structure.
- **Tags:** Quantum Information & Computing, Foundations & Interpretations

## 8. Altermagnetism on Hyperbolic Lattices
- **Authors:** Eric Petermann, et al.
- **arXiv ID:** 2605.10602
- **URL:** https://arxiv.org/abs/2605.10602
- **Categories:** cond-mat
- **Summary:** Extends altermagnetism — momentum-dependent spin splitting without net magnetization — to regular hyperbolic lattices in 2D via hyperbolic crystallography and band theory. Shows next-nearest neighbor hopping induces spin splitting in bipartite hyperbolic lattices. Identifies an entire family and special cases exhibiting altermagnetism. Since hyperbolic band theory yields at least 4D momentum space, classifies leading spin-splitting harmonics using 4D atomic orbitals.
- **Relevance:** Novel topological condensed matter physics on curved/hyperbolic lattices, expanding the landscape of quantum materials with nontrivial spin texture.
- **Tags:** Many-Body Physics & Condensed Matter, Phase Transitions & Critical Phenomena
