## 1. Effective spherical symmetry in Loop Quantum Gravity: A path integral approach
- **Authors:** Juan Carlos Del Águila, Hugo A. Morales
- **arXiv ID:** 2605.20814
- **URL:** https://arxivite.org/abs/2605.20814
- **Categories:** gr-qc
- **Summary:** This work derives a loop quantum corrected model for spherically symmetric vacuum spacetimes using the path integral method from Loop Quantum Cosmology. The authors find explicit corrections from inverse triad and holonomy effects that modify the Hamiltonian constraint. For inverse triad corrections, the modified geometry describes a black hole with a curvature singularity that does not lead to null geodesic incompleteness. Preliminary arguments suggest holonomy corrections may resolve the singularity entirely.
- **Relevance:** Direct application of LQG path integral techniques to spherically symmetric spacetimes — a key step toward understanding quantum black holes within the canonical quantization framework.
- **Tags:** loop-quantum-gravity, black-holes, path-integral, singularity-resolution

## 2. The fate of Reissner--Nordström--de Sitter black holes: nonequilibrium discharge and evaporation
- **Authors:** Damien A. Easson
- **arXiv ID:** 2605.20349
- **URL:** https://arxivite.org/abs/2605.20349
- **Categories:** hep-th, gr-qc
- **Summary:** Easson develops a semiclassical framework for RN-dS evaporation combining 2D dilaton gravity with Polyakov anomaly backreaction. The analysis yields closed adiabatic evolution equations for mass and charge. Analytically, neutral Schwarzschild-dS black holes lose mass monotonically. Schwinger pair production provides rapid discharge, and classical lukewarm/cold/extremal loci are not semiclassical attractors for controlled trajectories — the endpoint is empty de Sitter space.
- **Relevance:** A rigorous semiclassical treatment of charged black hole evaporation in de Sitter space, directly relevant to black hole thermodynamics and generalized second law studies.
- **Tags:** black-hole-thermodynamics, Reissner-Nordström, de-Sitter, evaporation, semiclassical-gravity

## 3. Two asymptotically flat spinning black holes balanced by their self-interacting, synchronised scalar hair
- **Authors:** Chen Liang, Carlos Herdeiro, Eugen Radu
- **arXiv ID:** 2605.20374
- **URL:** https://arxivite.org/abs/2605.20374
- **Categories:** gr-qc, hep-th
- **Summary:** The authors investigate balanced configurations of two spinning black holes with synchronised scalar hair (2sBHs), studying the effects of quartic scalar self-interactions. For 2sBHs, increasing the coupling reshapes the bifurcation structure; repulsive self-interactions cannot make horizons heavier, while attractive ones can. - **Relevance:** Multi-black-hole configurations with scalar hair are a frontier in beyond-Standard-Model black hole physics, with implications for gravitational wave signatures and stability.

## 4. Electromagnetic duality degeneracy in dynamical black hole mergers

- **arXiv ID:** 2605.20493
- **URL:** https://arxivite.org/abs/2605.20493
- **Categories:** gr-qc, hep-th
- **Summary:** This paper presents the first fully nonlinear realization of electromagnetic duality in dynamical strong-gravity regimes via numerical relativity simulations of charged black hole mergers. - **Relevance:** Establishes electromagnetic duality as an organizing principle for dynamical Einstein-Maxwell solutions, with implications for understanding degeneracies in gravitational-wave observables from charged compact objects.

## 5. Wasserstein Space of Quantum Chaos
- **Authors:** Koji Hashimoto, Norihiro Tanahashi, Kentaroh Yoshida
- **arXiv ID:** 2605.20995
- **URL:** https://arxivite.org/abs/2605.20995
- **Categories:** hep-th, gr-qc, quant-ph
- **Summary:** The authors find that the effective dimension of the Wasserstein space of energy eigenstates decreases as a quantum system becomes more chaotic. Using Husimi Q-representations and Sinkhorn-regularized optimal transport, they construct an embedding geometry that captures quantum scrambling via folding structures and detects quantum scars via branching. At the inverted harmonic oscillator separatrix, the Wasserstein distance correctly captures the Lyapunov exponent.

## 6. Topology sums, sectorwise holography, and horizon normalcy
- **Authors:** Naman Kumar
- **arXiv ID:** 2605.20294
- **URL:** https://arxivite.org/abs/2605.20294
- **Categories:** hep-th
- **Summary:** Kumar examines the "holography of information" principle when topology sums generate baby-universe sectors. If the baby-universe Hilbert space is nontrivial, HoI refines to an α-sectorwise statement rather than completeness on the full topology-summed space. In a fixed sector, HoI may obstruct AMPS factorization and allow a smooth horizon; in an unconditioned state, sector-independent smoothness requires aligned reconstructions or access to the sector label. The tension with Bousso's expectation of locally determined horizon normalcy is highlighted.
- **Relevance:** A subtle analysis of how baby-universe sectors affect firewall arguments and the holography of information — directly relevant to the black hole information paradox.
- **Tags:** holography-of-information, baby-universes, firewalls, black-hole-information, AMPS

## 7. Entangling Power: A Probe of Symmetry and Integrability in Quantum Many-Body Systems
- **Authors:** Ian Low, Pallab Goswami
- **arXiv ID:** 2605.20661
- **URL:** https://arxivite.org/abs/2605.20661
- **Categories:** quant-ph, cond-mat.str-el, hep-ph, hep-th, nucl-th
- **Summary:** The authors compute the entangling power of anisotropic Heisenberg spin chains and the two-magnon S-matrix. They establish a monotonic hierarchy: entangling power decreases as symmetry grows, reaching minimum at the SU(2) XXX point. Finite-size XXZ chains exhibit sharp dips at SU(2) points Δ=±1 and the free-fermion point Δ=0. In the thermodynamic limit, the S-matrix decomposes into quantum logic gates, with entangling power vanishing at SU(2) points and maximizing at the free-fermion point.
- **Relevance:** Entangling power serves as an operator-level diagnostic for symmetry and integrability in quantum many-body systems, with implications for quantum simulation of spin chains.
- **Tags:** entangling-power, quantum-many-body, spin-chains, integrability, symmetry

## 8. An Exponential Sample-Complexity Advantage for Coherent Quantum Inference

- **arXiv ID:** 2605.21457
- **URL:** https://arxivite.org/abs/2605.21457
- **Categories:** quant-ph
- **Summary:** This work studies coherent quantum inference where the desired output remains quantum rather than classical. For quantum purity amplification (QPA) with d-dimensional inputs, coherent processing achieves error ε using O(1/ε) copies, versus Ω(d/ε) copies for any incoherent protocol. The authors establish sharp coherent-incoherent separations and identify an entanglement-breaking limit that gives the optimal incoherent counterpart of each coherent protocol.
- **Relevance:** Demonstrates an exponential sample-complexity advantage for preserving coherence in quantum inference tasks, with direct implications for quantum computing algorithms and quantum state purification.

## 14. Thermodynamics of homogeneous Universes: de Sitter, Bonnor-Melvin and static Einstein

- **arXiv ID:** 2605.21047
- **URL:** https://arxivite.org/abs/2605.21047
- **Categories:** gr-qc, cond-mat.stat-mech
- **Summary:** Volovik constructs the thermodynamics of homogeneous universes (de Sitter, Bonnor-Melvin-Λ, and static Einstein) by treating the gravitational field as part of matter. ## 15. Universal fusion category symmetries on tensor products of infinite-dimensional Hilbert spaces
- **Authors:** Ian Bunner, Corey Jones
- **arXiv ID:** 2605.21327
- **URL:** https://arxivite.org/abs/2605.21327
- **Categories:** math-ph, cond-mat.str-el, hep-th, math.OA, math.QA
- **Summary:** The authors prove that anyon chains, after stabilizing with infinite-dimensional ancilla spaces, factorize locally as tensor products of infinite-dimensional Hilbert spaces. This implies any unitary fusion category can be realized as symmetries on such tensor products. They further show that any two anyon chains with the same symmetry category are related by a symmetry-compatible locality-preserving unitary, yielding a single stable equivalence class for each fusion category. As a corollary, Levin-Wen model boundary algebras are bounded-spread isomorphic after stabilization iff they share the same bulk topological order.
- **Relevance:** A foundational result on realizing arbitrary fusion category symmetries in infinite-dimensional Hilbert spaces, with direct implications for classifying topological order and Levin-Wen type models in condensed matter and quantum information.
- **Tags:** fusion-categories, topological-order, anyon-chains, Levin-Wen-models, symmetry
