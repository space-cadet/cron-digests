### 1. Holographic entanglement entropy in the QCD phase diagram under external magnetic field

**Authors:** Man-Man Sun, Man-Li Tian, Zhou-Run Zhu

**arXiv ID:** [2605.17438](https://arxivite.org/abs/2605.17438)

**Categories:** hep-th; hep-ph

**Summary:** This work explores holographic entanglement entropy (HEE) in the QCD phase diagram under an external magnetic field using an Einstein-Maxwell-dilaton model. For perpendicular magnetic field orientation, the entanglement entropy develops a swallow-tail structure indicating a transition between connected and disconnected entanglement surfaces. At large chemical potential, the entanglement entropy becomes multivalued but increasing the magnetic field restores single-valued behavior. These results are consistent with black hole thermodynamics and the QCD phase diagram, showing that entanglement entropy serves as an effective probe of the QCD phase transition.

**Relevance:** Directly connects holographic entanglement entropy, black hole thermodynamics, and QCD phase transitions — a rare intersection of Deepak's core interests.

**Tags:** holography, entanglement entropy, black hole thermodynamics, QCD phase transition, AdS/CFT

### 2. Bouncing singularities in Schwarzschild: a geometric origin of the QNM convergence region

**Authors:** Paolo Arnaudo, Benjamin Withers

**arXiv ID:** [2605.16489](https://arxivite.org/abs/2605.16489)

**Categories:** gr-qc; hep-th

**Summary:** The authors show analytically that the convergence of the quasinormal mode (QNM) expansion of the retarded Green's function in Schwarzschild spacetime is set by a singularity in the complex time plane. This "bouncing singularity" has a simple geometric origin — a null geodesic that bounces from the black hole singularity. This explains why the QNM convergence region at real times is bounded by a null ray scattering from r* = 0. The same singularities also create an annular convergence region for the Matsubara mode sum describing early-time behavior near the horizon.

**Relevance:** Provides a beautiful geometric interpretation of QNM convergence in terms of null geodesics bouncing from the singularity — foundational for black hole perturbation theory.

**Tags:** quasinormal modes, black holes, Green's function, null geodesics, bouncing singularity

### 3. Dynamical quasinormal mode excitation II: propagation and convergence in Schwarzschild

**Authors:** Marina De Amicis, Enrico Cannizzaro, Gregorio Carullo, Adrien Kuntz, Laura Sberna

**arXiv ID:** [2605.16492](https://arxivite.org/abs/2605.16492)

**Categories:** gr-qc; hep-th; math-ph

**Summary:** Building on their previous framework, the authors study dynamical QNM excitation during the plunge of a particle into a Schwarzschild black hole. They identify a new "bounce radius" r* = 0 as a characteristic radius for QNM excitation. To the right of this radius, the QNM signal scatters before reaching the observer; to the left, it propagates directly on the light-cone. The dynamical QNM signal undergoes a transition from a quasi-resonant regime (destructive interference of overtones) to a free-oscillator regime (rapid convergence) as the particle crosses the bounce radius. This provides a first-principles description of the waveform from shortly after the signal peak.

**Relevance:** Continuation of a highly relevant series on dynamical QNM excitation — directly applicable to gravitational-wave ringdown modeling and tests of general relativity.

**Tags:** quasinormal modes, gravitational waves, black hole perturbation, ringdown, waveform modeling

### 4. Dissipation-assisted preparation of Floquet-Laughlin states in superconducting circuits

**Authors:** Luis C. Steinfadt, André Eckardt, Francesco Petiziol

**arXiv ID:** [2605.18377](https://arxivite.org/abs/2605.18377)

**Categories:** quant-ph; cond-mat.quant-gas

**Summary:** The authors design protocols for driven-dissipative stabilization of fractional Chern insulator (FCI) ground states in superconducting circuits implementing the bosonic Harper-Hofstadter-Hubbard model. By coupling to driven leaky cavity modes that realize a tuneable artificial environment, they achieve the Floquet-FCI as an approximate fixed point. For systems of 2, 3, and 6 particles, they numerically demonstrate the detection of fractional quantum Hall signatures including bulk incompressibility, Hall response, and trapping of fractional charges. This provides a concrete pathway to dissipation-assisted preparation of strongly correlated states in quantum simulators.

**Relevance:** Bridges quantum computing hardware (superconducting circuits), topological order (Laughlin states), and driven-dissipative quantum simulation — highly interdisciplinary and timely.

**Tags:** quantum computing, topological order, fractional quantum Hall, superconducting circuits, Floquet engineering, dissipation

### 5. Effects of formation channels and gravitational lensing on stochastic gravitational wave background

**Authors:** Xin-yi Lin, Zhengxiang Li

**arXiv ID:** [2605.18097](https://arxivite.org/abs/2605.18097)

**Categories:** gr-qc

**Summary:** This work distinguishes between astrophysical black holes (ABHs) and primordial black holes (PBHs) through their contributions to the stochastic gravitational-wave background (SGWB). Using hierarchical Bayesian inference on GWTC-4 data, the authors infer PBH population hyperparameters and compute SGWBs from both channels, including lensing effects that modify strain amplitude. They find that both LIGO and the Einstein Telescope can distinguish ABH and PBH formation models in specific frequency ranges, with ET providing much broader discrimination power. Lensing alters the frequency range where discrimination is possible.

**Relevance:** Combines primordial black holes, gravitational-wave cosmology, and detector science — directly relevant to cosmological tests of gravity and dark matter.

**Tags:** primordial black holes, gravitational waves, stochastic background, lensing, Einstein Telescope, cosmology

### 6. Covariant Holographic Entanglement Entropy Inversion to Reconstruct Bulk Geometry

**Authors:** Ji-Seong Chae

**arXiv ID:** [2605.16459](https://arxivite.org/abs/2605.16459)

**Categories:** hep-th

**Summary:** The author presents an analytic inversion of covariant holographic entanglement entropy (HEE) that reconstructs the stationary radial bulk geometry including spatial warp factors and frame-dragging shifts. The interval entropy S(Δt, Δx) acts as an on-shell Hamilton-Jacobi functional, with endpoint derivatives giving conserved HRT charges E and J. For each fixed ratio κ = E/J, the entropy defines an Abel reconstruction of a candidate radial block. Cross-leaf agreement of candidates at different κ values provides a hidden integrability condition. When satisfied, the same data determine the projected bulk light cone, making frame-dragging, horizon generators, and ergosurfaces visible from entanglement.

**Relevance:** A significant advance in the bulk reconstruction program from holographic entanglement entropy — connects HRT formalism, integrability, and geometric reconstruction.

**Tags:** holography, entanglement entropy, bulk reconstruction, AdS/CFT, Hamilton-Jacobi, black holes

### 7. Rapidly Rotating Neutron Star Collapse in Massive Scalar-Tensor Theories

**Authors:** José Carlos Olvera M., Daniela D. Doneva, Pablo Cerdá-Durán, José A. Font, Stoytcho S. Yazadjiev

**arXiv ID:** [2605.16506](https://arxivite.org/abs/2605.16506)

**Categories:** gr-qc; astro-ph.HE

**Summary:** The authors present a full 3D numerical evolution code for neutron stars in massive scalar-tensor theories, embedded in the Einstein Toolkit. They examine gravitational collapse of rapidly rotating, scalarized neutron stars to black holes, finding an observational degeneracy in tensorial gravitational-wave emission between scalarized and GR collapses. However, this degeneracy is broken by scalar radiation carrying ~10^-3 M_sun c^2 of energy — orders of magnitude higher than quadrupolar emission (~10^-7 M_sun c^2). Rapid rotation enhances this signal since fast-rotating stars sustain larger scalar field amplitudes.

**Relevance:** Full 3D numerical relativity in modified gravity with scalar fields — directly tests scalar-tensor extensions of GR through gravitational-wave signatures.

**Tags:** numerical relativity, scalar-tensor gravity, neutron stars, gravitational collapse, gravitational waves, modified gravity

### 8. End-to-End Formalization of Quantum Error Correction

**Authors:** Mattias Ehatamm, Yi Lee, Xiaodi Wu, Runzhou Tao

**arXiv ID:** [2605.16523](https://arxivite.org/abs/2605.16523)

**Categories:** quant-ph; cs.LO

**Summary:** The authors present Lean-QEC, the first Lean 4 formalization of stabilizer-code theory delivering machine-checked distance certificates at industrial code sizes. They formalize the linear algebra of qubit states, Pauli group, stabilizer codes, binary symplectic representation, classical coding theory, and CSS/Bivariate Bicycle families. To break the combinatorial barrier, they translate distance conditions into Boolean satisfiability through a verified reduction, with BitVec-flattened encoding replacing Lean's Matrix representation. They obtain automatically-generated Lean-checked distance proofs for industrially viable qLDPC codes including [[90, 8, 10]] and [[70, 6, 9]] BB codes, scaling up to 144 qubits.

**Relevance:** Groundbreaking work on formally verified quantum error correction — essential for trustworthy fault-tolerant quantum computing and industrially relevant qLDPC codes.

**Tags:** quantum error correction, qLDPC, formal verification, Lean 4, stabilizer codes, CSS codes, Bivariate Bicycle codes

### 9. Particle Dynamics, Shadow and Hawking Sparsity of a Kalb-Ramond Black Hole Coupled to Nonlinear Electrodynamics

**Authors:** Faizuddin Ahmed, Ahmad Al-Badawi, İzzet Sakallı

**arXiv ID:** [2605.16461](https://arxivite.org/abs/2605.16461)

**Categories:** gr-qc; astro-ph.HE; hep-th

**Summary:** The authors study timelike and null geodesics of a static, spherically symmetric black hole sourced by a Kalb-Ramond field coupled to nonlinear electrodynamics. They derive closed-form expressions for effective potential, specific energy, and angular momentum of circular orbits. Analyzing the photon sphere, black hole shadow, and Lyapunov exponent for unstable null geodesics (which determines eikonal QNM frequencies), they compare shadow radii with EHT observations of M87* and Sgr A*. The Hawking sparsity parameter increases from the Schwarzschild value 16π³ ≈ 496 to nearly 1.7×10³, indicating a significantly sparser Hawking cascade.

**Relevance:** Explores exotic black holes with Kalb-Ramond fields and nonlinear electrodynamics — connects shadow observations, QNMs, and Hawking radiation sparsity.

**Tags:** black holes, Kalb-Ramond field, nonlinear electrodynamics, black hole shadow, quasinormal modes, Hawking radiation, EHT

### 10. Dyonic Black Holes in Lorentz-Violating Gravity with a Background Kalb–Ramond Field

**Authors:** Yu-Xuan Lin, Jia-Zhou Liu, Yu-Xiao Liu

**arXiv ID:** [2605.18371](https://arxivite.org/abs/2605.18371)

**Categories:** gr-qc

**Summary:** The authors construct an exact four-dimensional static, spherically symmetric dyonic black hole solution in Lorentz-violating gravity with a nonminimally coupled Kalb-Ramond field and electromagnetic field. They analyze geodesic motion, obtaining photon-sphere radius, shadow radius, and innermost stable circular orbit, showing that Lorentz-violating parameters and dyonic charges modify shadow size and stable motion domains. In extended phase space, they derive thermodynamic quantities, verify the first law and Smarr relation, and find a first-order phase transition between small and large black holes strongly influenced by Lorentz violation and dyonic charges.

### 11. The spacetime Penrose inequality under a quasi final state hypothesis

**Authors:** Ahmed Ellithy

**arXiv ID:** [2605.18730](https://arxivite.org/abs/2605.18730)

**Categories:** gr-qc; math.AP; math.DG

**Summary:** Penrose's original heuristic for the spacetime inequality relies on the black hole final state conjecture. This paper isolates a weaker but precise late-time condition called the quasi final state hypothesis and proves the spacetime Penrose inequality under it. For asymptotically flat globally hyperbolic spacetimes with a MOTS apparent horizon tube satisfying the dominant energy condition and the quasi final state hypothesis, every asymptotically flat initial data set whose boundary is a MOTS cross-section satisfies the spacetime Penrose inequality. The proof uses new "tangentially maximal" hypersurfaces governed by a quasilinear inward-parabolic PDE, where the spacetime Hawking mass reduces to the Riemannian Hawking mass.

**Relevance:** Major mathematical physics result — weakens the assumptions needed for the Penrose inequality proof, making it applicable to more general dynamical black hole spacetimes.

**Tags:** Penrose inequality, black hole thermodynamics, MOTS, apparent horizon, geometric analysis, spacetime geometry

### 12. Pole Skipping, Avoided Crossing, and Resonant Excitation in Kerr Quasinormal Modes near Algebraically Special Frequencies

**Authors:** Kei-ichiro Kubota, Hayato Motohashi

**arXiv ID:** [2605.17840](https://arxivite.org/abs/2605.17840)

**Categories:** gr-qc; astro-ph.HE; cond-mat.stat-mech; hep-th

**Summary:** Kerr quasinormal modes near algebraically special frequencies exhibit anomalous behavior including apparent bifurcation, disappearance, and nonsmooth connection to the Schwarzschild limit. Tracking poles and zeros of Green-function building blocks across Riemann sheets, the authors show that bifurcation is due to an avoided crossing accompanied by resonant excitation, while disappearance is due to pole skipping caused by cancellation of a QNM pole by a Matsubara-mode zero. This resolves long-standing puzzles about QNM behavior near special frequencies.

**Relevance:** Resolves decades-old puzzles about Kerr QNM anomalies — crucial for accurate gravitational-wave template construction and black hole spectroscopy.

**Tags:** Kerr black holes, quasinormal modes, pole skipping, avoided crossing, algebraically special frequencies, Green functions

### 13. Impact of sky localization uncertainty on ringdown inference

**Authors:** Kallol Dey, Enrico Barausse, Marco Crisostomi, Roberto Trotta

**arXiv ID:** [2605.18595](https://arxivite.org/abs/2605.18595)

**Categories:** gr-qc

**Summary:** As gravitational-wave ringdown signals grow louder, quasinormal-mode inference depends increasingly on treatment of extrinsic parameters. Standard analyses fix sky localization (and sometimes polarization and inclination) to point estimates from inspiral analysis, artificially breaking degeneracies and underestimating uncertainty. The authors test uninformative priors sampled jointly with remnant parameters, and informed priors from full signal posteriors. Both avoid bias from fixing sky localization, while mode amplitude ratios remain consistent — making them a robust observable for Kerr spectroscopy. Applied to GW250114 and GW190521, they confirm robust detection of the (2,2,1) overtone in GW250114.

**Relevance:** Important methodological advance for gravitational-wave ringdown analysis — impacts tests of the no-hair theorem and black hole spectroscopy with current and future detectors.

**Tags:** gravitational waves, ringdown, quasinormal modes, black hole spectroscopy, parameter estimation, LIGO

### 14. Revealing Hund superdispersion with tunneling spectroscopy

**Authors:** Luke C. Rhodes, Fabian B. Kugler, Olivier Gingras, Carolina Marques, Edgar Abarca Morales, Phil D.C. King, Antoine Georges, Peter Wahl

**arXiv ID:** [2605.16580](https://arxivite.org/abs/2605.16580)

**Categories:** cond-mat.str-el; cond-mat.mtrl-sci

**Summary:** In multi-orbital systems, Hund coupling creates qualitatively distinct "superdispersive" features in the spectral function that defy the conventional Mott-Hubbard paradigm. Using tunneling spectroscopy on Sr2RuO4, combined with DFT, dynamical mean-field theory, and continuum LDOS calculations, the authors reveal Hund-induced spectroscopic features. The experimental data are in excellent agreement with theoretical predictions and are intimately linked to the non-monotonous energy dependence of the real part of the self-energy in a Hund metal. This provides direct experimental evidence for Hund-induced features and opens a new route to probing correlation effects in quantum materials.

**Relevance:** Beautiful combination of experiment and theory revealing Hund physics in a canonical strongly correlated material — connects to superconductivity in ruthenates and cuprates.

**Tags:** strongly correlated electrons, Hund coupling, tunneling spectroscopy, Sr2RuO4, dynamical mean-field theory, superconductivity

### 15. Exact Organization of Density Matrices and Entanglement Structure in the Kitaev Spin Liquid

**Authors:** Chen-Chih Wang, Yi-Ping Huang, Sungkit Yip

**arXiv ID:** [2605.18002](https://arxivite.org/abs/2605.18002)

**Categories:** cond-mat.str-el

**Summary:** The authors derive the exact density matrix of the spin-1/2 Kitaev spin liquid in terms of spin operators and study its entanglement structure. The density matrix is organized by equivalence classes of string operators associated with the underlying gauge structure. Using the exact Gauss law and 1-form Wilson symmetry, they demonstrate a symmetry-resolved block-diagonal structure of the reduced density matrix responsible for extensive degeneracy in the entanglement spectrum. This block-diagonal structure explains the separability of entanglement entropy into gauge and matter parts. The formalism is extended to subsystems with odd numbers of sites, revealing a relation between entanglement spectrum and fermion parity rarely discussed in the literature.

**Relevance:** Exact results on entanglement in the paradigmatic Kitaev spin liquid — foundational for topological order, quantum spin liquids, and gauge-gravity duality analogues.

**Tags:** Kitaev model, quantum spin liquid, topological order, entanglement entropy, gauge theory, exactly solvable models
