## 2. On the Nature of Entangling Photons in Horizon-Induced Decoherence
- **Authors:** Max Joseph Fahn, Alessandro Pesci
- **arXiv ID:** 2605.19588
- **URL:** https://arxivite.org/abs/2605.19588
- **Categories:** gr-qc, quant-ph
- **Summary:** Analysis of how a Killing horizon induces decoherence on a quantum system in superposition of states, focusing on electrically-charged systems. Photons crossing the horizon become entangled with the external system, carrying away which-path information and causing decoherence. The effect is related to the Unruh temperature and provides a microscopic mechanism for horizon-induced quantum-to-classical transitions.
- **Relevance:** Bridges quantum information theory with black hole physics. The explicit mechanism linking horizon geometry to decoherence has implications for black hole information paradox, firewalls, and the nature of quantum measurements near horizons.

## 3. Quasinormal Modes of Proca and Maxwell Fields in d-Dimensional Schwarzschild-AdS Black Holes
- **Authors:** David C. Lopes, Tiago V. Fernandes, José P. S. Lemos
- **arXiv ID:** 2605.18947
- **URL:** https://arxivite.org/abs/2605.18947
- **Categories:** gr-qc, astro-ph.HE, hep-th
- **Summary:** Investigation of linear perturbations and quasinormal modes (QNMs) of Proca and Maxwell fields in d-dimensional Schwarzschild-AdS black holes with Dirichlet boundary conditions. The Proca field equations reduce to one decoupled and two coupled second-order differential equations, while Maxwell fields decouple completely. The quasinormal spectra are computed for various dimensions and horizon radii, revealing how field spin and spacetime dimension affect the ringdown signature.
- **Relevance:** Higher-dimensional AdS black holes are central to holography and brane-world scenarios. Understanding vector-field QNMs is essential for gravitational-wave phenomenology and AdS/CFT applications where gauge fields probe the bulk geometry.

## 4. After the Fluid: Subexponential Decay in AdS₄
- **Authors:** John R. V. Crump, Jorge E. Santos
- **arXiv ID:** 2605.18978
- **URL:** https://arxivite.org/abs/2605.18978
- **Categories:** hep-th, gr-qc
- **Summary:** Study of late-time behavior of nonlinear perturbations of Schwarzschild-AdS₄ black branes. Real-analytic initial data generically enter a regime controlled by the large-k tail of the quasinormal mode spectrum. The asymptotic scaling of Im(ω) with mode numbers leads to subexponential (stretched exponential) rather than pure exponential decay, representing a breakdown of the fluid-gravity correspondence at very late times.
- **Relevance:** Challenges the conventional fluid-gravity correspondence by showing that nonlinear AdS perturbations exhibit fundamentally different late-time behavior. This has implications for thermalization in strongly coupled field theories and the validity of hydrodynamic descriptions in holography.
- **Tags:** AdS/CFT, Black Hole Perturbation, Fluid-Gravity Correspondence, Late-Time Decay, Nonlinear Dynamics

- **URL:** https://arxivite.org/abs/2605.19036
- **Categories:** cond-mat.str-el, cond-mat.supr-con, quant-ph
- **Summary:** Demonstration that a non-interacting gas of charge-e/3 anyons with exchange statistics θ = −π/3 can superconduct through a hidden weak-pairing mechanism. Such anyons arise in doped fractional Chern insulators at filling 1/3 or 2/3, where projective lattice translations enforce three-fold anyon condensation. ## 13. Deconfined Boundary Phase Transition of a Quantum Critical Heisenberg Model
- **Authors:** Chengxiang Ding, Long Zhang
- **arXiv ID:** 2605.19011
- **URL:** https://arxivite.org/abs/2605.19011
- **Categories:** cond-mat.str-el, cond-mat.stat-mech
- **Summary:** Investigation of boundary phases of a (2+1)-dimensional quantum critical Heisenberg model with a dangling spin chain. Introducing a multispin Q-term along the boundary drives a continuous transition from antiferromagnetic order to valence-bond solid order. Large-scale quantum Monte Carlo simulations reveal that the transition is deconfined — governed by an emergent O(4) symmetry and described by a non-compact CP¹ field theory — rather than following the conventional Landau-Ginzburg-Wilson paradigm.
- **Relevance:** Deconfined quantum criticality is a major paradigm shift in condensed matter physics, showing that not all continuous phase transitions are Landau-type. The boundary version studied here is novel and connects to high-energy physics through the non-compact CP¹ description.
- **Tags:** Deconfined Quantum Criticality, Phase Transition, Heisenberg Model, Quantum Monte Carlo, Valence-Bond Solid

## 15. Nearly Perfect Fermi Surface Nesting in Hole-Doped La₃Ni₂O₇ Enables Bulk Superconductivity Without Pressure or Strain
- **Authors:** Chengliang Xia, Jiale Chen, Hongquan Liu, Hanghui Chen
- **arXiv ID:** 2605.19297
- **URL:** https://arxivite.org/abs/2605.19297
- **Categories:** cond-mat.supr-con, cond-mat.mtrl-sci, cond-mat.str-el
- **Summary:** First-principles calculation revealing that hole-doped La₃Ni₂O₇ exhibits nearly perfect Fermi surface nesting, which drives a bulk superconducting instability at ambient pressure without strain. The bilayer Ni-O structure produces a unique Fermi surface topology with strong nesting vectors that couple electron and hole pockets, generating a spin-fluctuation-mediated pairing interaction. The calculated critical temperature is consistent with experimental observations under pressure.
- **Relevance:** A significant theoretical advance for bilayer nickelate superconductivity — showing that the same mechanism responsible for pressure-induced superconductivity can work at ambient pressure with hole doping. This opens a practical route to stable, high-Tc nickelate superconductors.
